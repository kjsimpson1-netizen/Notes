We can express the change in [[Chemistry/Entropy/Entropy|Entropy]] for any reaction by finding the standard state of entropy using the 3rd Law of Thermodynamics, and show us what the change in entropy of the reaction will be. 
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta S^\circ_{\text{reaction}} = \sum S^\circ_{\text{products}} - \sum S^\circ_{\text{reactants}}
\end{aligned}
$
$
