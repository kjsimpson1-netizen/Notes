There is also a **standard [[Gibb's Free Energy]]**, and this is the elements at their standard state. This is the same idea as [[Standard Molar Entropy]].
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta G^o&= \sum\limits v_p\cdot\Delta G^o(\text{Products})-\sum\limits v_p\cdot\Delta G^o(\text{Reactants})
\end{aligned}
$$
We can then use this with [[Standard Molar Entropy]] and [[Chemistry/Thermodynamics/Standard Enthalpy]] with standard free energy. We can represent this as:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta G^o&= \Delta H^o-T\Delta S^o
\end{aligned}
$$
