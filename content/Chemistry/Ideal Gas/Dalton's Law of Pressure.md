This is the concept that the total pressure of a gas is the summation of all the partial pressures.
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\text{Total}&= P_1+P_2+P_2 ...
\end{aligned}
$$
We can then use this in the ideal law:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P_\text{total}V&= n_\text{total}RT\\
P_1V&= n_1RT
\end{aligned}
$$
This is directly proportional to the [[Mole Fraction]], provide the temperature and the volume both remain constant. If the temperature and the volume are both constant, we can express the ratio as the following:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{n_1}{n_1+n_2+n_3...}&= \frac{P_1}{P_1+P_2+P_3...}
\end{aligned}
$$
