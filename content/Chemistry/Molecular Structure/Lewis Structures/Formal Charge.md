$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Formal Charge}=\text{Valence Electrons}-\text{Lone Pair Electrons}-\frac12\text{Bonded Electrons}

$$
$$
The overall sum of the formal charges is the charge of the molecule that they are a part of
