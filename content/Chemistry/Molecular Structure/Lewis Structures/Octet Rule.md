### Octet Rule
This states that every atom wants to be surrounded by 8 [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Valence Electrons]]. This is because this is how many electrons that are in the valence shell of noble gasses, and since they have the most favourable Potential Energy, this is what the molecules desire to be. 
### Sextet Rule
Sometimes if necessary, the group 13 molecules will have a total of 6 [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Valence Electrons]].
### Duet Rule
This states that helium and hydrogen only want 2 [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Valence Electrons]]. This is because they only have a 1s orbital, and don't have any more space the any extra electrons.
