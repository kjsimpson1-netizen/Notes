This is the total amount of bonding sites around a molecule. The steric number of anything in period 1 and 2 of the [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Periodic Table]] can be a maximum of 4, but lower periods that have [[Chemistry/Molecular Structure/Lewis Structures/Hyper Valence]] can have the steric number of 7. Both lone pairs and bonded pairs count towards the steric number, and the lone pairs actually end up taking more space.
