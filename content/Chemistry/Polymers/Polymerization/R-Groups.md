These are side chains from the main backbone of the [[Chemistry/Polymers/Polymers]]. They are not a part of the polymerization, rather they are just the additional parts of the monomer that can determine it's function, but they will not determine how the [[Chemistry/Polymers/Polymers]] will form
>[!Warning]
>These R-groups can have an effect on the [[Chemistry/Intermolecular Forces/Intermolecular Forces]] between the polymer chains, causing it to form secondary, tertiary and quaternary structures
