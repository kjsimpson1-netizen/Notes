These are the parts of a monomer that will react in a [[Condensation Polymer]] together with other [[Functional Groups]] to form the polymer chain. The most common [[Functional Groups]] are the two acids, carboxylic acid and acyl chloride, as well as two other functional groups being amine and alcohols.
