A polymer is a chain of monomers that are connected together by a sequential stringing together of smaller molecules, that are all identically shaped. If the polymer is relatively short (<20 monomers), this is called an oligomer.
In order for the polymer to form , there must be two different bonding ends of a monomer that will connect to each other. Or, there can be multiple monomers that have the same ends, however the ends of each of the monomers must be complimentary. 
There are two types of polymers, a [[Condensation Polymer]] and an [[Addition Polymer]].

## Different Number of Monomers
### 1 monomer Strategy
This is when there is a single monomer with two [[Functional Groups]] that each will be able to [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Bond]] with each other. For this strategy, the repeat unit is the single monomer.
### 2 monomer Strategy
This occurs when there is another monomer necessary to form the polymer, as the first monomer lacks the complimentary pair to the other portion. For this strategy, the repeat unit is the two monomers together
## Degree of polymerization
This depends on the number of repeat units in the chain. We can find the number of units by taking the total mass of the entire polymer, then divide it by the overall mass of the monomer.
