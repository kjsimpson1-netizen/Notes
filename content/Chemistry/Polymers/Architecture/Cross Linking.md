This occurs when there can be [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Covalent Bonds]] formed between other [[Chemistry/Polymers/Polymers]], that are created by an additional chemical. 
## Flexibility
This causes very flexible, but also very strong polymers. 
## Strength
Also, cross-linked polymers will have a very high average molecular mass, giving it more strength. This will also increase thermal stability. 
## Elasticity
The more crosslinking that is present in a polymer, the less elastic it will be and the stiffer that is will be, however, up until a point there can still be plenty of elasticity. 
>[!Note]
>Vulcanization is an example of cross linking, when sulphur is added to the polymer which will allow covalent bonds to form between the molecules.
