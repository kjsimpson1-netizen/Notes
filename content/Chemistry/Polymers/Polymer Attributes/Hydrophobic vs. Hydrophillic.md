The part of the polymer structure that will determine how a polymer will react when exposed to water depends on the polymers [[Chemistry/Polymers/Polymerization/R-Groups]]. 
### Hydrophobic
Anything that has non-polar [[Chemistry/Polymers/Polymerization/R-Groups]] will not attract water, as water is polar, and like attracts like.
### Hydrophilic
This is any polymer that has ionic or polar [[Chemistry/Polymers/Polymerization/R-Groups]], as these will attract the polarity of water and will therefore make it hydrophilic.
