This relates the [[Equilibrium Constant]] to the temperature:
$$\begin{align*}
\ln\left(\frac{K_2}{K_1}\right)&= -\frac{\Delta H\degree}R\left(\frac1{T_2}-\frac1{T_1}\right)\\
\hline\\
K&= \text{Equilibrium Consant}\\
\Delta H\degree&= \text{Standard Enthalpy}\\
T&= \text{Temperature}
\end{align*}$$
>[!Note]
>This equation has the assumption that the [[Chemistry/Thermodynamics/Standard Enthalpy]] does not change with temperature
