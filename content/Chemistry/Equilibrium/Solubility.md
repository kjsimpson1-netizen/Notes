Solubility is the ability for a solute to dissolve into a given solvent to form a solution. The constant of solubility is represented by $K_{sp}$ and it will dictate how much of the solute can dissolve into the solvent. This is calculated the same way as the [[Equilibrium Constant]] is calculated, and it follows the same principles. 
>[!Note]
>The solid solute that cannot be dissolved into the solution is called a precipitate

The precipitate state occurs when the [[Chemical Potential Energy]] of the solution is lower for the solid rather than for the solution, and then the precipitate will be more favourable to be formed rather than to remain in solution.
