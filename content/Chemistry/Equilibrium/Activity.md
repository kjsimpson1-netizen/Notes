This is the effectiveness of the chemical undergoing reaction, with the higher activity implying a higher activity in the reaction. There are several different equations for activity for each state of matter:
>[!Note]
>The $\gamma$ is assumed to be 1 for Chem-154. We can also just know to use the equation for the [[Reaction Quotient]] and then just place the reaction coefficient out front
## Gasses
$$\begin{align*}
a_x&= \gamma_x\frac{P_x}{P_0}\\
\hline\\
a&= \text{Activity}\\
\gamma&= \text{Activity Coefficient}\\
P&= \text{Pressure}
\end{align*}$$
## Solutions
$$\begin{align*}
a_x&= \gamma_x\frac{[x]}{M_0}\\
\hline\\
a&= \text{Activity}\\
\gamma&= \text{Activity Coefficient}\\
M&= \text{Concentration}
\end{align*}$$
## Solids and Liquids
For solids and liquids, their activities are always approximately 1, so they are generally ignored in the [[Reaction Quotient]] and [[Equilibrium Constant]].
