This is when there is a reaction where the forward and the reverse reactions are exactly equal, and the reaction can be expressed as being in equilibrium. This is indicated by the $\rightleftharpoons$ symbol.
>[!Note]
>The size of the arrows in the symbol can indicate which of the reactions is going faster
