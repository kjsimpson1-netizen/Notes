This is represented by the symbol $Q$ and this is the quotient of the products over the reactants.
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&= \frac {\text{[Products]}}{\text{[Reactants]}}
\end{aligned}
$$
If this quotient is less that the [[Equilibrium Constant]] of the reaction, then this reaction will progress towards the products, and if the quotient is greater than the [[Equilibrium Constant]], then it will shift towards the reactants. When the reaction quotient and the [[Equilibrium Constant]] are equal, then the reaction is at equilibrium.
