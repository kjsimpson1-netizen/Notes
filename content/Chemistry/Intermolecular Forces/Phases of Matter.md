As the temperature of a substance increases, the substance shifts from solid, then from liquid to gas. As we move from solid to liquid to gas, the [[Intermolecular Forces]] decrease, the molecular spacing increases and the [[Chemistry/Entropy/Entropy|Entropy]] increases. 
### Definition
A distinct phase is when the whole substance is when all the physical properties are consistent throughout the substance (an ice cube in water is not a distinct phase). A phase change occurs when the substance changes from one substance to another. 
A one-component system is when the substance is composed of a single, pure substance.

When there is no net macroscopic flow of heat, the system is in thermal equilibrium and there is no net work. This works with other changes as well, such as density and pressure and temperature, when there is no change in any of these, then that property is in equilibrium.
