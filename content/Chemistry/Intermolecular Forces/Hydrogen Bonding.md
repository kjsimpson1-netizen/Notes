This is a stronger sub-category of [[Dipole-Dipole Forces]]. For it to be defined as hydrogen bond, the hydrogen **must** be attached to fluorine, oxygen or nitrogen, any other molecule will not work. If there are hydrogen bonds present, they will trump all other [[Intermolecular Forces]] (provided that the other forces don't have higher quantities). The hydrogen bonds must also be between two hydrogens that are bound to one of the three mentioned molecules, if both do not have [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Covalent Bonds]] to one of the three, there will be no hydrogen bond.
