This is the collection of the [[Elementary Steps]] that form the final simplified reaction, or the net reaction.
## Rate Determining Step
This is the [[Elementary Steps]] that take the longest time, and it is the one that limits the progression of the reaction
## Reaction Intermediates
These are the chemicals that are made between the [[Elementary Steps]], however it is used up in the next one, and does not appear in the net reaction
## Catalyst
These are chemicals that are present in the initial reactants, and also in the final products, as the catalyst is not used up during the reaction. They will present an alternate reaction mechanism with a smaller lower [[Activation Energy]]
## Transition States
Theses are the peaks of the graph
