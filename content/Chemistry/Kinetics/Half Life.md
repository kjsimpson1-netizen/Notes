This is the time for which the concentration of the reactant has been half used. This is useful because it is independent of the initial concentrations or quantities
>[!Note]
>You can also just solve the [[Arrhenius Equation]] while inputting that the second concentration is half of the first
## Zeroth Order
$$
\begin{aligned}
t_\frac12&= \frac{[A]_0}{2k}\\
\hline\\
k&= \text{Reaction Constant}
\end{aligned}
$$
## First Order
$$
\begin{aligned}
t_\frac12&= \frac{\ln(2)}{k}\\
\hline\\
k&= \text{Reaction Constant}
\end{aligned}
$$
