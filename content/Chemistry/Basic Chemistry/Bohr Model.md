The Bohr model of the atom originally described the hydrogen atom. It was discovered by Niels Bohr, and he found that:
- Atoms are neutral (positive and negative charge)
- Atoms are stable ([[Chemistry/Coulomb Forces]] must be balanced with something else, Bohr thought angular momentum)
- Electrons are in well defined [[Orbits]]
However, Bohr made this model before the particle-wave duality of matter was found, and he neglected that the position of an electron can not be found with 100% certainty, so instead of there being a discrete orbit, there are fuzzy [[Orbitals]] in reality. This is why the Bohr model looks like a solar system, as he believed that the electrons were like planets orbiting the sun.
