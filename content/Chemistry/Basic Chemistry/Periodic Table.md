The elements were grouped as they appear today originally based upon their reactivity (e.g. noble gasses are inert). The columns of the periodic table are called groups, and the rows are called periods.
An atom is made of protons, neutrons and electrons, each of the three that have their own charge and mass. The atomic mass unit ($1.66\times10^{-27}\;\text{kg}$) was defined by $\frac1{12}$ of the mass of the carbon atom. The electron mass is so small compared to that of the proton and neutrons that it is negligible compared to that of the other two.
