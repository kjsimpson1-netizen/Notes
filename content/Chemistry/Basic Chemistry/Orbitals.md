## Orbitals
These are the wave representation of electrons in an atom. This represent the probability of where the electrons will be found, and each orbital becomes larger as the energy level increases. 
## Pauli Exclusion
This states that in each of the orbitals, there are a maximum of two electrons, but each electron must have a different spin to exist in the same orbital, either spin up or spin down.

## Hund's Rules
The electrons are filled into the shells and subshells from the lowest number to the highest. 
While filling the orbitals, each subshell is originally filled with one electron (generally spin up), before being filled with their down spin pair electron

| Shell | Sub Shells | Max Electrons |
| ----- | ---------- | ------------- |
| 1     | s          | 2             |
| 2     | s,p        | 8             |
| 3     | s,p,d      | 18            |
| 4     | s,p,d,n    | 32            |
