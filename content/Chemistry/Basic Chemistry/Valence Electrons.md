## Valence electrons
The valence electrons are the ones on the outermost electron shell, and these are the freest electrons. The ones nearer to the nucleus are called core electrons. 
The valence electrons are the ones that are more important in chemical bonding, as they are more reactive. The transition metals have many exceptions, but the s and p blocks of the [[Periodic Table]] are very consistent when it comes to filling their valence shells.
