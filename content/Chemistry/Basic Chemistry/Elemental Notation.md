For any given element, there are a few different symbols. They take the following form:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
^A_ZE
\end{aligned}
$
$
## A
This is the atomic mass number, and this is the number of protons plus the number of neutrons, also known as nucleons. 
## Z
This is the number of protons, or the atomic number of the element
## E
This is the elemental symbol
