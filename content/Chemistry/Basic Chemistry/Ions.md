An ion is any atom that has either too many, or too few electrons from it's usual configuration. This will cause a charge across the entirety of the atom. If there is an electron missing, this will cause there to be a positive charge due to the lack of a negative charge, and this is called a **cation**. If there are too many electrons, there will be an overall negative charge and this is an **anion**. The electrons that are exchanged to make it an ion are the [[Valence Electrons]]
Ions are very important in things such are [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Ionic Bonds]] and [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Ionic Compounds]].
