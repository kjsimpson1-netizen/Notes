This is the equation that relates the standard cell potential to the temperature, the amount of [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Moles]] of electrons being transferred and the [[Chemistry/Equilibrium/Reaction Quotient]]. These are for non-idealized batteries without all solutions being at standard values. 
$
$
\begin{aligned}
E_\text{cell}&= E_\text{cell}^o-\frac{RT}{nF}\ln(Q)\\
\hline\\
E_\text{cell}&= \text{Cell Potential}\\
R&= \text{Gas Constant}\\
T&= \text{Temperature}\\
n&= \text{Moles}\\
F&= \text{Faraday's Constant}\\
Q&= \text{Reaction Quotient}
\end{aligned}
$
$
