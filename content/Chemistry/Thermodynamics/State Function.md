This states that the change in any state function is independent upon how you get there, it just matters in the difference between the initial state and the final state. There is many other things that are state functions, such as:
- Temperature
- Pressure
- Volume
- Internal Energy
- Enthalpy
- Entropy
- Gibb's Energy
>[!Note]
>The variables that have a capital letter generally will have a state function rather than [[Path Functions]].
