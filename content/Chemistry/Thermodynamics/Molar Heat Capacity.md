Sometimes rather than talking about large amounts of matter, we want to talk about smaller quantities, and using kilograms for this can be annoying, so rather we can use [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Moles]] of matter as our quantity. 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&= nC\Delta T
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
With the $n$ specifying how many [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Moles]] of the molecule are present, and with the $C$ denoting the molar heat capacity of the material. 
>[!Note]
>These measurements are usually made at constant atmospheric pressure
