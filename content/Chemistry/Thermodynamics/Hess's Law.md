The sum of the change in [[Enthalpy]] for all steps gives the overall reaction [[Enthalpy]]. This is true because [[Enthalpy]] is a [[State Function]], and it does not matter what path the species takes. We can also state that if the reaction runs in reverse, that the sign of the $\Delta H$ will be the opposite. And since heat and [[Enthalpy]] are extensive properties, we must scale it with the quantity of moles as well.
