Electronegativity is represented by either $EN$ or $\chi$ This is the ability of an atom to attract shared electrons to itself in [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Covalent Bonds]]. A greater electronegativity means that there is a greater [[Electron Affinity]] and a higher tendency to pull electrons closer. This is not an atomic property because it refers to atoms in a [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Bond]], not just by themselves. This is a calculated property. Also, the higher the difference in electronegativity, the more polarized that an atomic [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Bond/Bond]] is.
### Across a period
As we go across a period, the electronegativity increases because the [[Atomic Radius]] decreases while the [[Effective Nuclear Charge]] increases.
### Down a group
As we go down a period, the electronegativity decreases because the [[Atomic Radius]] increases while the [[Effective Nuclear Charge]] increases, but less so. This means that the electrons will have a smaller pull towards the nucleus.
