These are the bonds that are present in [[Covalent Compounds]] and it is where the [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Valence Electrons]] are shared between the two atoms present in the compound. These are weaker than [[Ionic Bonds]] because they share the electrons, rather than with the [[Ionic Compounds]] being attracted to each other through [[Chemistry/Coulomb Forces]] after having completely take the other electron. 
>[!Note]
>The electrons that are shared in these pairs are known as **bonding pairs** or **shared pairs**

Covalent bonds will form if the atoms interacting have an [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Periodic Properties/Electronegativity]] difference that is less than $1.7$. This is because if the difference in [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Periodic Properties/Electronegativity]] is greater than this, then the more electronegative atom will just steal the electron rather than share it, and it would be an [[Ionic Compounds]].
