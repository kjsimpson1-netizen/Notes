Lattice energy is the amount of energy that it takes to completely ionize [[Ionic Compounds]] into a gaseous state. The two factors that effect the lattice energies are the magnitude of the charge on the ion, with higher charges having a higher lattice energy due to the stronger pull of the nucleus to attract/repel more electrons. The other factor that effects this is the [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Periodic Properties/Atomic Radius]], as the larger it is, the less pull that it will have on the [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Valence Electrons]] and therefore making the amount of energy required to separate them decrease.
