These are compounds in which the electrons are not shared equally, and rather the atom with higher [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Periodic Properties/Electronegativity]] will steal the electron from the other. This leaves both of the atoms with full [[Chemistry/Basic Chemistry/Valence Electrons]] shells. However, the two species will form one cation and another anion, and it is this difference in charges that allows the compound to stick together with [[Chemistry/Coulomb Forces]], unlike the electrons like in [[Covalent Compounds]].
