In some [[Covalent Compounds]], the electrons are not equally shared ([[Electron Density]] is higher in one atom than in another) due to differences in [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Periodic Properties/Electronegativity]]. This will cause dipoles on the [[Covalent Compounds]], causing them to be attracted with [[Chemistry/Intermolecular Forces/Dipole-Dipole Forces]]. 
A [[Bond]] can also be polar, but this does not necessarily have to make the entire molecule polar if the rest of the molecules is symmetrical. General polarity is caused by asymmetries in the charge on the molecule.
