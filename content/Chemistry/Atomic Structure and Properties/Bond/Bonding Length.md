This is the distance between any two nuclei of atoms in a [[Bond]].
If we get two hydrogen molecules close to each other, the electron of the other molecule then feels the [[Chemistry/Coulomb Forces]] of the nucleus. The two nuclei will approach to the point where the attraction to the other electron will feel the greatest attraction to the nucleus, while also feeling the least repulsion from the other electron, as well as the two nuclei repelling each other the least possible amount. They will then form a [[Bond]] and create the $H_2$ [[Covalent Compounds]], which has a lower overall Potential Energy, and therefore it is the most energetically favourable.
Bond length is also inversely proportional to [[Bond Order]], so the higher the [[Bond Order]], the shorter the bond length, as there is a greater pull.
