These are just [[Chemistry/Coulomb Forces]] that are present between the ions that make up the [[Ionic Compounds]]. These are stronger than [[Covalent Bonds]], as the electrons aren't shared and rather just stolen by the ion with higher [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Periodic Properties/Electronegativity]]. 
In order for an ionic bond to exist, the difference in [[Chemistry/Atomic Structure and Properties/Periodic Properties/Electronegativity]] must be greater than or equal to 1.7 in order to have a large enough difference to steal the electron rather than sharing it.
