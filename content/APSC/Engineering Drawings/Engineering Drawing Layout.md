### Third-Angle Projection
We can just use the front, the top and the end view to accurately represent any object. Usually the front view is shown in the middle, the top view is shown above and the right end view is shown to the right. 
>[!Note]
>All the different views should be aligned with their respective vertices of other faces
### Title Blocks
This is in the lower right corner, and contains all the important information for the part such as the date, the drawing name, the drawing number, the drawing scale, information for who prepared the drawing and when, as well as units for the measurements
### Dimensions
These are measurements that allow us to see the sizes of the parts. These are shown as pure numbers with no units, as the units are in the title block. 
#### Dimension Lines
There are dimension lines that show the measurement being made between the extension lines from the part itself. These lines should not be placed inside the part, and rather outside. Also, these dimensions should be placed between views so that similar measurements are more easily associated. It is also preferable to show visible dimensions, not hidden ones. 
>[!Note]
>Extension lines do not touch the part and do not show redundant measurements by over dimensioning
