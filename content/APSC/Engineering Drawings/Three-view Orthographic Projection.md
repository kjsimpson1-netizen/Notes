The idea of this is to show 3 of the faces of the object. 
### Hidden Lines
These are lines that are not apparent on the object as we are looking at it, but they are necessary to be able to accurately represent the object in only 3 views. These are represented with dashed lines. 
>[!Note]
>Circular features are usually identified with centre marks and centre lines. These are represented by long-short-long lines
