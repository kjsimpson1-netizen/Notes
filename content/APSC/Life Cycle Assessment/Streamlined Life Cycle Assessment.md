This is a more qualitative version of the [[life cycle assessment]] that is used earlier on in projects when the exact data is not available. It is more common to do an SLCA with a SLCA matrix rather than a graph, as each of the cells can be summed to give a environmentally responsible product rating ($R_{ERP}$).
## Common Uses
We can use the $R_{ERP}$ values to compare different options in terms of performance, or we can assess different areas of negative impact in the overall life cycle
