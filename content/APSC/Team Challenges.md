## Equity
>[!Definition]
>Equity is making sure that all members have the same opportunities and outcomes, whereas equality is treating all people the same, regardless of any other reasons (valid or invalid)
## Diversity
It is important to recognize and value the unique backgrounds that different people who value different points of value bring to the table. Having diversity will increase the functionality of a team as there are many different points of view.
### Biases
These are the stereotypes drawn from different dimensions of diversity, and this can cause the unequal treatment of some people compared to others. These biases come from our lived experiences which can unconsciously form biases, called implicit bias. 
### Microaggression
These are brief and common statements and actions that have unintentional messages. 
### Stereotype Threat
This occurs when there is a stereotype that applies to a person, and they then become self conscious as to not fulfill that stereotype. 
## Inclusion
This is the addressing of inequities of people of different background, identities and experiences and to build a diverse community
