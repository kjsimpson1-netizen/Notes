## Avoiding
This is when the [[conflict]] is ignored. This is used when it is more useful to deescalate situations and think things through.
## Accommodating
This is being flexible so that you give up your needs so someone else gets theirs. This is good when the issue is important to them but not to you.
## Competing
This involves pushing to see that your goals are met, even if this means that others do not get what they want. This is good when the issue is important to you but not to them.
## Compromising
This is where both of the parties give a little and get a little to meet in the middle. This is good when time is limited and team relations should be balanced. 
## Collaborating
This is when all parties work together to find a solution that meets all the needs. This is ideal when there is plenty of time to work towards everyone's ideal solution.
