These are used to represent the satisfaction of [[Evaluation Criteria]]. This is the same as the weighted score in a [[Weighted Decision Matrix]], however the scale may be different. 
>[!Note]
>If there is a [[Requirements]], then there is a no satisfaction there below the value at which the requirement occurs

These lines don't have to be linear, rather they generally are a S-curve. The greatest satisfaction per unit of [[Evaluation Criteria]] occurs where the slope is the greatest. The shape comes from understanding the market. 
## Design Parameter vs. Attributes
The design parameters are the inputs which are determined by engineers, and the product attributes are the outputs which are viewed by the [[Stakeholders]].
