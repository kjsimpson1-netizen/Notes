### Avoid
This is the best way to deal with risk, and it is to change what is being done to avoid the [[risk]] completely
### Mitigate
This is when the risk cannot be avoided, and rather the severity of the [[risk]] can be reduced
### Transfer
This is when the [[risk]] can be passed onto someone else (i.e. insurance)
### Accept
This is done when the risk cannot be avoided, mitigated or transferred, and rather the [[risk]] must just be lived with
