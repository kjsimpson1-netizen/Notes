The overall risk is the product of the likelihood of the risk to occur and the severity of the risk to have the _possibility_ to cause harm. There are a variety of different [[Sources of Risk]] as well as [[Risk Types]] which can be categorized in a risk identification table, each one with it's own [[Risk Management Strategies]]. We can determine which strategy would be best used by quantifying the risk using a [[Risk Matrix]], then they can be delt with using a [[Risk Management Process]].
