While making [[Presentations]], it is important to know who your audience is, such as what they need to know, what they already know, and what they do not know. It is important to know these things in order to format your presentation around this in order to get the maximum impact.
These things also impact how complex or simple your presentation is, and what kind of jargon you can use without having to define key terms. 
It is also important to know what the opinion of your presentation that the audience has. 
>[!Note]
>Audiences are not always of just one type of person, rather they are composed of many different groups of opinions, and knowledge levels with respect to your presentation.
