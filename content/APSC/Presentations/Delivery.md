This is the art of conveying your voice through voice and body language. This includes posture, eye contact, gesture, and speech. The delivery of the presentation often has a direct impact on the [[Audience]] attention and takeaway. 
## Body Language
It is important to not stand completely still, and move around and have expressions, as well as hand gestures. This is called being <u>expressive</u>. It is also important to have constant eye contact with the audience to remain engaged, as well as also not just reading off of notes. Confidence also played an integral role, as it is difficult to focus and connect with the presenter is they are stiff and fidgety.
>[!Note]
>Don't over do it though, as this can also be distracting

Your gestures will also depend on the size of your audience. In general, the larger that your audience is, the larger your gestures will be as well. 
## Professionalism
It is important to dress professionally as to be taken serious, as well as to have a professional attitude while presenting your presentation. It is also important to have enthusiasm while presenting, it is also important to not have too much and appear too animated. 
### Attire
This will have a direct impact on how authoritative you seem, and how serious that your audience will take you. Typically, dress as well as or slightly better than your audience. For this presentation, dress as if you're attending a job interview. 
## Language
Don't use overly complex jargon if you're audience doesn't yet have a firm grasp on it. If it is necessary to use a certain word, define it first before using it. This can cause you to come across as pretentious. Also, don't speak as if you're speaking to your peers and friends, keep it formal, which will take away from the persuasiveness.
