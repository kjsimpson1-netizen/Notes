This determines the goals and reasons behind your presentation and the outcome you hope to achieve. Having a clear sense of the purpose of your presentation will allow you to have a clear idea of what information you need to include, and how you need to convey this information. Generally, the purpose of your presentation tend to fit into one of two purposes:
## Inform
This is when you're giving information to your [[Audience]] in order to inform them what the purpose of your presentation is
## Persuade
This is when you are trying to convince your [[Audience]] that you're right or the right option to choose. 
>[!Note]
>It is very possible to have a mixed purpose in your presentation, it does not necessarily have to be one of the two listed above.
