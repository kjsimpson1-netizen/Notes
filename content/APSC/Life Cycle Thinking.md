This is a type of [[Systems Thinking]]. This goes beyond immediate impacts, to focus upon each stage of the lifespan of the product. The steps of life cycle thinking are:
1. Extraction of raw materials
2. Design and production
3. Packaging and distribution
4. Use and maintenance
5. End of life
>[!Note]
>At the end of life phase, the waste can either be thrown away, recovered to make raw material, recycled into new products, or best, reuse the product in it's current state.
