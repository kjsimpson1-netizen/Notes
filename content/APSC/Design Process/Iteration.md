This is the idea of revisiting and redoing the [[Design Process]] over and over again as we learn more about the problem, returning to various different steps until a final solution is developed. There is no limit to how many times that a [[Design Process]] can be iterated. 
>[!Note]
>It is best that we keep a record of all of our ideas and concepts, as if we iterate and need to return to a certain concept, we will need a record of it to refer back to
