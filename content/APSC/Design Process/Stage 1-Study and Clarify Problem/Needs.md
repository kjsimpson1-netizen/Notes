These are wants or expectations of the what the final project should be able to do, in order to satisfy the [[Stakeholders]]. Each group of [[Stakeholders]] may have a different needs, however there can still be overlap. We can also note that not all [[Stakeholders]] from the same group think alike, as some needs for someone aren't a need for another. 
## Threshold Needs
These are needs that must be met in order for the [[Stakeholders]] to be satisfied. Often, [[Stakeholders]] are very aware of these needs, however are unlikely to mention them if asked as they are thought of as obvious. We must dig deeper when asking questions to determine these.
## Expressed Needs
These are needs that [[Stakeholders]] are very likely to mention, as they are very aware of them. When asked what a need for a product is, this is likely what they will mention.
## Latent Needs
These are needs that [[Stakeholders]] have not considered, or did not think possible. Satisfying a latent need will often make [[Stakeholders]] very happy. We will also need to dig very deep into questions to get stakeholders to give these.
