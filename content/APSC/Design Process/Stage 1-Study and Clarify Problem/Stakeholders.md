A stakeholder is any who has influence on the project, or is influenced by the project. Each stakeholder will have different degrees of influence on a project (for example, if the government doesn't allow it, it won't happen). 
However, knowing how much power a stakeholder has isn't always clear, as if a seemingly negligible group bands together, they can form a stakeholder with a large degree of influence. We can rank how much influence a stakeholder has, we can look at their degree of [[Salience]].
In order to minimize these risks, we must look at all the issues and the opinions of the stakeholders from the beginning. 
It is impossible to make every stakeholder happy, but we must be able to know how much each stakeholder will effect the project.
>[!Note]
>A stakeholder who has additional legal or human rights that could be impacted by a project are considered **rights holders**, and should be treated with extra caution.
