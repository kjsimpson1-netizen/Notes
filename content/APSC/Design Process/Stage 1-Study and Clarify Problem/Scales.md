These are the different breadths of context that come into an engineering project. These are a part of an engineering mindset and a way of thinking, not just a specific tool. There are scales for:
1. Spatial
2. Temporal
3. Organizational
Just thinking about scales doesn't solve the problem for us, but it does allow us to figure out what scale is best suited to solve the problem. It also allows to see different potential ranges of [[Stakeholders]] that we may have missed otherwise.
