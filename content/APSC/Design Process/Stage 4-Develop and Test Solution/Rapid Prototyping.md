This is generally any type of cad prototyping that are turned into physical [[Prototypes]]. This is a way to quickly move between virtual and physical prototypes. The three main types of rapid prototyping are 3-D printing, waterjet cutters, and lase cutters.
## 3-D Printer
This is a printer that deposits very thin layers of plastic, layer by layer to form the final part. These are better than the other two modelling types because they can be done in 3 dimensions. 
>[!Warning]
>These can create dangerous vapours as well as airborne particles

- FDM (fuse deposition modelling) is the classic type of 3D printing that lays down layers of melted plastic. This is difficult to make overhanging pieces, as they will tend to droop.
- SLS (Selective laser sintering) uses laser to melt layers of thin plastic to form the 3D object. This is better than FDM in basically every way
## Waterjet Cutter
This is when any sheet of material is cut using a water jet containing small amounts of sand that will cut through almost anything. The downside to these is that the head can only point straight down, so it can only be used on 2D shapes. Also, it will cut through the entire depth, there is no way to control this. It can also only be used on material that can get wet
## Laser Cutting
This is when there is a laser that is used to cut through the sheets of materials. The materials are generally softer things, such as cardboard or plastics or wood, not usually metals or glass (however, these can be done with a more specialized tool). These can also be used to etch or to engrave the parts.
