This is the step in the [[Design Process]] after screening and ranking. During this step, we will take whichever of the ideas that ranked the highest based off of their [[Evaluation Criteria]] that we got based off of the [[Needs]] of the [[Stakeholders]], and consider them more carefully and quantitatively. We do not care about the requirements for this step, as it is assumed that they were screened out in the [[Screening]] process.
These more detailed considerations are done using either [[Prototypes]] or more detailed trials or simulations.
>[!Note]
>Because this stage of the [[Design Process]] is time and cost intensive, we don' want to bring too many ideas forward

In the end, we are looking for the one idea that we will carry forward to the develop and test stage.
