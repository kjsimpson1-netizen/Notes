All teams go through conflict, the difference is how it is delt with. How it is delt with often depends upon the [[Team Development]]. 
## Forming
Conflict is often avoided and ignored
## Storming
The conflict is in the open, and often impedes the work of the team
## Norming/Performing
Conflict is resolved quickly and in a way that does not endanger relationships.
# Moving between stages
## Forming$\rightarrow$Storming
This requires the team working together and getting to know each other. 
## Storming$\rightarrow$Norming
This requires openness of communications and interactions and a desire of the team to improve
## Norming$\rightarrow$Performing
This requires a safe environment to make the team members feel comfortable
