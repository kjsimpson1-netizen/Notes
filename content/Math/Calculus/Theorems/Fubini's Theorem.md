This theorem states that under the right conditions, we can take a [[Double Integrals|Double Integral]] and turn it into an [[Iterated Integral]] and do one variable at a time rather than multiple at once. 
$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{x=a}^b\left(\int_{y=c}^df(x,y)dx\right)dy\equiv\int_{y=c}^d\left(\int_{x=a}^bf(x,y)dx\right)dy
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>It is helpful to explicitly state with what variable the [[Bounds]] of integration are being done with respect to in order to prevent confusion
