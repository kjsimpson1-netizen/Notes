This is very similar to the [[Fundamental Theorem of Calculus]], however in 3 dimensions. This will give us the same theorem for the [[Gradient]], giving us:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\int_C\nabla T(r)\cdot d\vec r&= T(b)-T(a)
\end{aligned}$$
$$
Since we are evaluating the curve $C$ from the points $a$ to $b$, if $a=b$, this gives us that the work is 0 (see the [[Line Integral Theorem]]).
