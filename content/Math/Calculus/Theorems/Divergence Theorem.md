  The divergence theorem states that for a closed surface, the net flow through the surface is equal to the total source generation inside of the closed surface. This allows us to rewrite a [[Surface Integral|Flux Integral]] as rather the [[Integral]] of a [[Divergence]]:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
&\iint_S F\cdot N\;dS= \iiint_D(\nabla \cdot F)dV\\
\hline\\
S&= \text{Closed Surface around Volume }V\\
N&= \text{Outward Normal Vector of surface }S\\
F&= \text{Vector Field}\\
V&= \text{Volume inside }S
\end{aligned}$$
$$
