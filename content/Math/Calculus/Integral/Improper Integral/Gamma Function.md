The gamma function is defined by the [[Improper Integral]]:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\Gamma (t)&= \int_0^\infty x^{t-1}e^{-x}dx
\end{aligned}$$
$$
It can be proved that the gamma function can also be expressed as:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\Gamma(n)&= (n-1)!
\end{aligned}$$
$$
Which is equivalent to the [[Factorials]] function shifted by one.
