A secant line is any line joining two different values of a graph, giving the average rate of change over that [[Interval]].
For example, this is the secant line between $x=1$ and $x=3$ for the function $f(x)=x^2$.
 ![[SecantLine.png]]
We can find the secant line by first finding the [[Slope]] of the graph using [[Point Slope form]]:
$$
\begin{aligned}
m&= \frac{y-f(a)}{x-a}
\end{aligned}
$$
Using this [[Slope]], we can plug it into another equation with just one of the two pairs of points that we entered:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{y-f(a)}{x-a}&= m\\
y&= m(x-a)+f(a)
\end{aligned}
$$
This will then give us the secant line between the two points.
