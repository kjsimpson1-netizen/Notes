The tangent line is the line that has the exact perfect [[Slope]] of the line and barely touches the graph at the given value
## Equation
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
y=f(a)+f'(a)\cdot(x-a)
\end{aligned}$$
$$
This gets more complicated for other functions, such as $y=\sin(x)$ and $y=x(x-1)(x+1)$. We can do this by first sketching the graphs. The tangent line does usually just barely touch the graph, but in some cases it can cross the graph at other points as well.

