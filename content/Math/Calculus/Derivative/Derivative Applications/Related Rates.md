This is when there are various variables related with an equation (e.g. the inflation of a balloon). These can have many variables, for this example just the radius of the balloon, but this can get very complicated. With these examples, when we have the equation for the relation we can take the [[Derivative]] of both of the sides of the equation with respect to time, using [[Implicit Differentiation]].
>[!Note]
>It is important to declare the variables, as well as the units.
