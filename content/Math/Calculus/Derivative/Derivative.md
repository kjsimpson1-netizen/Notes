This is the equation that represents the rate of change of a function. There are many derivative rules that can be used to make differentiation easier, but it's simplest form is done using the [[Derivative Definition]]. The derivative is the [[Slope]] of the function. 
There are a few different notations for the derivative, such as [[Lagrange's Notation]] and [[Leibniz Notation]], but they all mean the same thing; however, there are some benefits to using each of them.
