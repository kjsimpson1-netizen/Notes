This is used for when there is a [[Derivative]] that is being taken in the form of a quotient
## Formula
### Leibniz Notation
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{v\frac{du}{dx}-u\frac{dv}{dx}}{v^2}
\end{aligned}
$
$
### Newtonian Notation
$
$
\begin{aligned}
f'\left(\frac uv\right)&= \frac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}
\end{aligned}
$
$
