When doing [[Integral|Integration]] with multivariable functions, we are allowed to integrate with only one of the variables, also known as a partial integral. This is a integral part to being able to take [[Double Integrals|Double Integrals]], because it is just a repeated partial integral. 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
g(y)=\int_{x=a}^{b}f(x,y)dx
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
This is done by treating all variables that are not with respect to the integral as constants.
