This is very similar to [[Polar Integration]], however now in 3 dimensions. We can think of this as first taking the an object with radially symmetry and projecting it down onto the plane into a circular shape:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\iint_Rr\int f(r,\theta,z) dz\;dr\;d\theta
\end{aligned}$$
$$
>[!Note]
>For any $x$'s or $y$'s that are in the equation, replace them with $x=r\cos\theta$ and $y=r\sin\theta$ respectively. $z$ holds it's own value
