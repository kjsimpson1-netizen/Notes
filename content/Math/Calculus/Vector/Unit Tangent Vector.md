This is the [[Unit Vector]] that points in the same direction as the [[Vector Derivative]]. This can be found by taking the direction of the [[Vector Derivative]] and dividing it by the [[Norm]] of the [[Vector Derivative]]:
$$\begin{align*}
\hat T(t)&= \frac{\frac d{dt}\vec r(t)}{|\frac d{dt}\vec r(t)|}
\end{align*}$$
