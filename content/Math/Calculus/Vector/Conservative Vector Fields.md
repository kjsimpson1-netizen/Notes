A [[Vector Field]] is conservative if the [[Curl]] of the [[Vector Field]] is 0 everywhere, and the [[Domain]] that it exists upon is [[Simply Connected]]. A conservative vector field implies that any [[Line Integrals|Line Integral]] taken upon it is invariant of path, and only the  [[Bounds]] of the [[Line Integrals|Line Integral]] matter. This also implies that any closed loop is equal to 0 (this is where the difference between a [[State Function]] and a [[Path Functions]] arise).
