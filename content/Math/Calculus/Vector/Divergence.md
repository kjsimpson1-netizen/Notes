This is the [[Dot Product]] equivalent of [[Curl]]. This is done by summing all of the [[Partial Derivatives]] of a [[Vector]] rather than taking their [[Cross Product]]. This is done by:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{div}\bf{F}&= \nabla\cdot\bf {F}=\bf {F}_x+\bf F_y+F_z
\end{aligned}$$
$$
The physical interpretation of this is that this is the rate at which fluid is being created or destroyed at each point. If the divergence at a point is positive, then this implies that fluid is being created, and negative implies it is being destroyed.
