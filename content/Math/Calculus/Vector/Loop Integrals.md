These are a [[Line Integrals|Line Integral]] where the two [[Bounds]] are the same. However, unlike regular [[Integral|Integrals]], this does not necessarily imply it is zero. It is only equal to zero if the [[Vector Field]] is [[Conservative Vector Fields|Conservative]], and otherwise it will have a non-zero value. These take the form:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\oint_C\vec f\;d\vec r
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
>[!Note]
>In order for this to apply, the [[Curl]] must be equal to 0.
