These are very similar to functions, however instead of taking in one value and then outputting another value, a transform will take in one function and then it will output another function. This is used in things such as the [[Laplace Transforms]]. These will take the general form:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
F(s)=\int_\alpha^\beta K(s,t)f(x)dx
\end{aligned}$$
$$
Here, $K(s,t)$ is the [[Nullspace|Kernel]]
