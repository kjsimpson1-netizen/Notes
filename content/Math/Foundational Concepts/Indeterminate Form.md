This occurs when there is an expression that cannot be evaluated because it has a paradoxical nature, such as $\frac00$ or $\frac\infty\infty$. These expressions cannot be evaluated, and rather must be factored or have a special tool applied to make the output be determinable.
We can evaluate this using:
- [[L'Hopitals Rule]]
- [[Taylor Series]]
- [[Horizontal Asymptotes]]
- [[Math/Analysis/Series/Series]]
