This is the formula that relates all the important constants and operations of math. The official expression of Euler's formula is:
$$\begin{align*}
e^{it}&= \cos t+i\sin t
\end{align*}$$
We can express the functions sine and cosine as functions of exponentials of [[Imaginary Numbers]]:
$$\begin{align*}
\cos(t)=\frac{e^{it}+e^{-it}}2\qquad\sin(t)=\frac{e^{it}-e^{-it}}{2i}
\end{align*}$$
If we evaluate this at $t=\pi$, we get:
$$\begin{align*}
e^{\pi i}-1&= 0
\end{align*}$$
