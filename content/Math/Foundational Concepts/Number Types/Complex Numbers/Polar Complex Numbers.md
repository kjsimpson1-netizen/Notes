 For any [[Complex Numbers]], because it is a two dimensional space, we can also represent it as a polar coordinate. This form is useful as it is easier to compute the products and quotients of [[Complex Numbers]].
$$\begin{align*}
z_1z_2=r_1r_2\cdot e^{i(\theta_1+\theta_2)}\qquad \frac{z_1}{z_2}=\frac{r_1}{r_2}e^{i(\theta_1-\theta_2)}
\end{align*}$$
## How to Convert?
We can take it from the form:
$$\begin{align*}
z=a+bi
\end{align*}$$
We can then change this into the form through [[Euler's Formula]]:
$$\begin{align*}
z&=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)=re^{i\theta}
\end{align*}$$
Where:
$$\begin{align*}
r&= |z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}\qquad\theta=\arctan\left(\frac ba\right)
\end{align*}$$
With $\theta$ being between $-\pi$ and $\pi$.
>[!Note]
>There is also a multivalued argument function $\arg$ which will give out all valid outputs
