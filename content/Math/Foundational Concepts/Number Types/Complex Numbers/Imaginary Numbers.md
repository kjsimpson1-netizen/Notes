## Euler's Expansion
This is extremely important to math, and it is:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
re^{i\theta}&\\
\\
e^{i\theta}&= \cos\theta+i\sin \theta
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
We can use this to solve other problems, for example:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
e^{i\left(\frac\pi2\right)}&= \cos\left(\frac\pi2\right)+i\sin\left(\frac\pi2\right)\\
&= 0+i\\
&= i
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
### Euler's Identity
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
e^{i\theta}&= \cos(\pi)+i\sin(\theta)\\
&= -1+0\\
&= -1\\
e^{i\theta}+1&= 0
