These are a combination of [[Imaginary Numbers]] and [[Real Numbers]], represented by $\mathbb{C}$. These are found in the form $a+bi$, where $a$ and $b$ are [[Real Numbers]]. For a complex number $z$, the real part is represented by $Re\{z\}$ and the imaginary part is $Im\{z\}$. To find each part, we can do the following:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
Re\{z\}=\frac{z+\bar z}2=a\qquad Im\{z\}=\frac{z-\bar z}{i}=b
\end{aligned}
$
$
Which then gives us the form $a\pm bi$.
## Complex Conjugate
When there is a complex number with a bar on it ($\bar a$), this is the conjugate of the complex number. To do this, we just flip the sign of the imaginary portions
## Length of a Complex Number
We can find the length of a complex number using the following:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
|z|&= \sqrt{z\bar z}=\sqrt{a^2-(bi)^2}
\end{aligned}
$
$
## Division
To do the division of complex numbers, we can do the following:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{z_1}{z_2}=\frac{z_1\bar{z_2}}{|z_2|^2} =\frac{(a_1a_2+b_1b_2)+(a_2b_1-a_1b_2)i}{a_2^2+b_2^2}
\end{aligned}
$
$
