These can be expressed as a ratio between any [[Polynomial Function]], and is of the form:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
R(x)&= \frac{P_1(x)}{P_2(x)}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
This means that rational functions are defined over all reals, provided that the denominator, $P_2(x)$ in this example, does not equal 0 as this would be a division by 0 and result in an [[Infinity]]. We can use principles of a [[Limit]] to find the [[Horizontal Asymptotes]] and [[Vertical Asymptotes]].
