## Definition of a Power Function
A power function takes the form:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
f(x)&= Kx^n
\end{aligned}$$
$$
Where k and n are constants
### If...
$K=0$, then $f(x)=0$
$n=0$, then $f(x)=K$
## Even and Odd power functions
Depending on whether or not the power is an even or odd integer, we can determine it's form. If it is an even number, then it will have a curve mirrored about it's vertex, or the y-axis if there is no horizontal translation, whereas odd powers will be flipped over their x-axis as well. More formally, we can state this as:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
&\text{Even Power Function}\\
f(x)&= f(-x)\\
\\
&\text{Odd Power Function}\\
f(x)&= -f(-x)
\end{aligned}$$
$$
All power functions are also unbounded upon their x-axis, meaning that they are defined and they [[Continuity]] along the entirety of the domain.

We can also state that odd power functions are 1 to 1, or that every x value has a unique y-value, whereas with even power functions, every y-value has two corresponding x values. This also means that odd power functions span the entire range of real numbers, whereas even power functions only span half. 
## Roots
Because a root can also be represented by a power function, they also obey all the same rules, however a square root of a negative of a even root cannot be taken, therefore the domain on those is $[0,\infty)$.
