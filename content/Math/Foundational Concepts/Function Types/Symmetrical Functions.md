A function can either have odd symmetry, even symmetry, or no symmetry whatsoever.
## Even
We can say that the function has even symmetry if:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(-x)=f(x)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
## Odd
We can say that a function has even symmetry if:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(-x)&= -f(x)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
---
Some functions that generally have these types of symmetry are:
- [[Power Function]]
- [[Trigonometric Functions]]
- [[Absolute Value Functions]]
- [[Polynomial Function]]
