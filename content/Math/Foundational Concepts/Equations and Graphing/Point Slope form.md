This is the form that makes it easiest to evaluate points, and can be seen as a way to evaluate the slope of [[Secant Lines]]. It takes the form:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{y-f(a)}{x-a}&= m
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
This is also a more useful form for taking the [[Derivative]] of the function, as we can squeeze the run, or the $x-a$ infinitely small.
