This is very useful for finding the vertex of a parabola, which can be very useful for taking the [[Integral]] of parabolas. The equation for completing the square from the base form into vertex form is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
ax^2+bx+c&= a\left(x+\frac b{2a}\right)^2+\left(c-\frac{b^2}{4a}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
