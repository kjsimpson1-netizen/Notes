This is the rate of change of a function at any point, it can also be thought of as the steepness of the graph. It can be found using either the [[Derivative Definition]] evaluated at that point or just [[Secant Lines]]. In general, when the slope is positive, the graph goes up to the right, when it is negative, it goes down to the left. Whenever it is flat, the slope is 0 and whenever it is vertical, the slope is [[Infinity]] so it does not exist.
