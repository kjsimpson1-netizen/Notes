A factorial is any number multiplied by all the [[Integers]] below it. This can be represented as:
$
\begin{aligned}
n!=\prod_{i=1}^ni
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>If we are dividing a factorial by another factorial, we are effectively erasing the overlapping terms, therefore we can express this as:
>$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{(n+b)!}{(n+a)!}=\prod_{i=a+1}^bn+i
\end{aligned}
$
