This is taking an original [[Logical Statements]] and taking the opposite, usually using "not". Example is "it is Tuesday" has the negation "it is not Tuesday". For a logical statement $P$, the negation is ~$P$. The [[Truth Tables]] for this is:

| $P$ | ~$P$ | ~(~$P$) |
| --- | ---- | ------- |
| T   | F    | T       |
| F   | T    | F       |
>[!Note]
>The negation of a negation is strictly the same as the original statement (unlike spoken English).
