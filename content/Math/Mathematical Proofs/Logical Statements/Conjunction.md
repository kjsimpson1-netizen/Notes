This is the combination of two [[Logical Statements]] to have their output depend upon the two values. This can be done using the word "and". The conjunction of two [[Logical Statements]] ($P$ and $Q$) can be shown with $P\land Q$. This is true when both $P$ and $Q$ are true. The [[Truth Tables]] for this is:

| $P$ | $Q$ | $P\land Q$ |
| --- | --- | ---------- |
| T   | T   | T          |
| T   | F   | F          |
| F   | T   | F          |
| F   | F   | F          |
