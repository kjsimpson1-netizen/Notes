This the long term average, or the mean, of the distribution of [[Probability]]. This is given by the function:
## Discrete Values
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}=\sum x\cdot\text{Pr}(X=x)
\end{aligned}
$$
## Continuous Values
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}&= \int_{-\infty}^\infty xf(x)dx
\end{aligned}
$$
