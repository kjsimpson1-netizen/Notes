In order for a function of [[Complex Numbers]] to be considered analytic, they must meet the following two criterion:
1. $f(x,y)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)$ where $u$ and $v$ exist and are continuous
2. [[Cauchy-Riemann Equations]] hold
>[!Note]
>In order for this function to exist, it must have both real and imaginary parts, and this implies that we cannot have any [[Complex Conjugate]] present, i.e. $\frac{\partial f}{\partial \bar z}=0$ must hold
>
