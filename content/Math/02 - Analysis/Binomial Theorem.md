This is the theorem for the expansion of a binomial to the nth degree. We can represent this with the [[Summation Notation]]:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A+b)^n&= \sum\limits_{k=0}^{n}\begin{pmatrix}n\\k\end{pmatrix}a^{n-k}b^k
\end{aligned}
$$
We can also use pascals triangle to determine the coefficients of the terms.
