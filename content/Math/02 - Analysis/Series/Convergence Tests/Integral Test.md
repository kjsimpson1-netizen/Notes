For this test, the function must be decreasing and greater than 0 in the bounds.
## Convergence
The series and the integral still share the same convergence behaviour, and therefore if one converges, the other one will converge as well. 
## Bounding
We can also bound the values for which the sum must be. Whether or not the [[Series]] or the [[Integral]] will be larger depends on the indexing. In general, whichever one has a larger starting index will be small than the other, as it is the same as "shifting" the series or integral to the left. However, if the index is the same, then the series will be larger
