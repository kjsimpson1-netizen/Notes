When there are multiple terms with uncertainty having mathematical operations done on them, the uncertainty will change. There are different equations for this, or the general form can be used, from which the rest of the equations are derived. The general form of this equation is:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\delta y(x_1,x_2...x_n)&= \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n\left(\frac{\partial y}{\partial x_i}\cdot\delta x_i\right)^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
Where the [[Partial Derivatives]] of each term multiplied by the uncertainty makes up each of the terms. This one equation can be used to derive the rest of the uncertainty equations.
