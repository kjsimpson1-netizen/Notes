## Digital Uncertainty
For a digital uncertainty, we can express the uncertainty by:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_d&= \frac{a}{\sqrt3}
\end{aligned}
$
$
Where "$a$" is the maximum uncertainty given.
## Analog Uncertainty
This is similar to the analog uncertainty, which is given by:

$
$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma_a&= \frac{a}{\sqrt6}
\end{aligned}
$
$
Where "$a$" is the maximum uncertainty given.
