This is the spread of a [[Probability Density Function]]. This can be calculated using the [[Expected Value]], using one of the following equations:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Var}(x)&= \mathbb{E}(X^2)-\mathbb{E}(X)^2
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
The higher that this value is, the more spread out that the data will be. The spread for variance will be given in the square of the units, as both of the terms for the [[Expected Value]] are squared. 
We can also express the variance as:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Var}(X)&= \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}(x-\mathbb{E}(X))^2\cdot f(x)\,dx
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
