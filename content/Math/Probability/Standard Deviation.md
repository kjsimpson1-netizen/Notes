This can be calculated using the [[Variance]] of a [[Probability Density Function]]. Whereas with the [[Variance]] which has the square of the units, the standard deviation will have the same units as the original function. This tells us how far away from the [[Expected Value]] that most of the values will fall. 
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma(X)&= \sqrt{(\text{Var}(X))}
\end{aligned}
$
$
