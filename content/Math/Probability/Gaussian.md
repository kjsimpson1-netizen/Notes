This function is the basis to the bell curve, and it takes the form:
$$\begin{align*}
f(x)&= \frac1{\sqrt2\pi}e^{-\frac{x^2}2}
\end{align*}$$
One of the reasons why this function is special is because it has no analytical antiderivative, rather the antiderivative is expressed as the [[Error Function]]. This also is used in [[Probability]] distributions and for standard distributions, and is very important in probability mathematics.
