In order to solve a non-[[Homogeneity|Homogenous]] [[System of Differential Equations]], we must include a particular [[Matrix]] (this is very similar to finding the regular particular solutions). This can be found using the equation:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\vec x_p(t)&= X(t)\cdot\int X^{-1}\vec f(t)dt
\end{aligned}$$Where $X(t)$ is the $n\times n$ matrix of the complimentary solution, and $\vec f(t)$ is the forcing term from the problem.
$$
