When solving [[Partial Differential Equations]] using the [[Power Series Solutions]], if there is a [[Singular Point]] and it follows that:
$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha=\lim_{x\to x_0}(x-x_0)\frac{Q(x)}{P(x)}\quad\text{ and }\quad\beta\lim_{x\to x_0}(x-x_0)^2\cdot\frac{R(x)}{P(x)}
\end{aligned}
$
Are both **finite**. If either of these values are **infinite**, then it is considered a **irregular [[Singular Point]]**.
