This is similar to the [[Characteristic Equation]], however it is usually applied to [[Euler Equations]]. This will take the form of:
$$
\begin{aligned}
r^2+(\alpha-1)r+\beta=0
\end{aligned}
$$
Where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are defined as:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha=\lim_{x\to x_0}(x-x_0)\frac{Q(x)}{P(x)}\quad\text{ and }\quad\beta\lim_{x\to x_0}(x-x_0)^2\cdot\frac{R(x)}{P(x)}
\end{aligned}
$$
