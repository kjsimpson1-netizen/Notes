This is used when sketching trajectories of a [[System of Differential Equations]]. This can be calculated using the following matrix:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
J&= \begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\partial f(x_0,y_0)}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial f(x_0,y_0)}{\partial y}\\
\frac{\partial g(x_0,y_0)}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial g(x_0,y_0)}{\partial y}
\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
Where $x_0$ and $y_0$ are the locations of the critical points of the system when sketching trajectories.
