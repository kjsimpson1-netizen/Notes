If an [[Ordinary Differential Equations|ODE]] is a linear function of $y,\dot y,\ddot y...$ then the ODE is linear, however if it is not, then it is non-linear. 
### Examples
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\text{Non-Linear}&\rightarrow yy'+y^2= 1\\
\text{Linear}&\rightarrow y''+t^2y= 1\\
\text{Non-Linear}&\rightarrow y'= \sin(y)
\end{aligned}
$
$
## General ODE form
A general linear ODE of order $n$ can be written as:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{k=0}^{n} a_k(t)\, y^{(n-k)}(t) &= q(t)
\end{aligned}
$
$
