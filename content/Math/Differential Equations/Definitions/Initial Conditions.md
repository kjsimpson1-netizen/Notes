This is the value that is given that is used to find the constant in any [[Ordinary Differential Equations|ODE]], as that value is lost during integration. These are needed to get a specific, non homogenous solution to an [[Ordinary Differential Equations|ODE]] as otherwise it is just a general solution. 
>[!Note]
>For an $nth$ [[Order]] [[Ordinary Differential Equations|ODE]], we will need $n$ different initial values

Also, the unique solution that the initial solution gives us must lie on the [[Interval]] on which the function exists. This is because the solution cannot jump across [[Vertical Asymptotes]].
