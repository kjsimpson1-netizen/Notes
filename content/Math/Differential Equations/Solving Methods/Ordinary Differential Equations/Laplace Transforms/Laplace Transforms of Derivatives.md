We can also take the [[Laplace Transforms]] of [[Derivative|Derivatives]] (this is useful in using [[Laplace Transforms]] to solve [[Ordinary Differential Equations]]):
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\hline\\
&\textcolor{red}{\text{First Derivative}}\\
\mathcal{L}\{g'(t)\}(s)&= s\mathcal{L}\{g(t)\}(s)-g(0)\\
\hline\\
&\textcolor{red}{\text{Second Derivative}}\\
\mathcal{L}\{g''(t)\}(s)&= s^2\mathcal{L}\{g(t)\}(s)-g'(0)-sg(0)\\
\hline
\end{aligned}$$
$$
