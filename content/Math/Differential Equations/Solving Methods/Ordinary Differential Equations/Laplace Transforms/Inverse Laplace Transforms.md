Linearity also applies to [[Laplace Transforms]], where the inverse is defined to be the function that takes the [[Laplace Transforms]] and will output the original function. This is often done by taking a function in the form $F(s)$, where $F(s)$ is a [[Rational Functions|Rational Function]], and using [[Partial Fractions]] to break it down into terms that we recognize.
>[!Note]
>If we can't break down the denominator due to the expression having [[Complex Numbers]] as the roots, we must complete the square
