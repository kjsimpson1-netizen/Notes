These [[Ordinary Differential Equations|ODE]] will take the form of:
$$\begin{align*}
\frac{dy}{dx}&= f(x,y)
\end{align*}$$
For the simplest kind, $y'(x)=f(x)$, we can simply take the [[Antiderivative]] to find the answer, so the solution is $\int f(x)dx+C$. We are often given the [[Initial Conditions]] in these problems that allow us to find the value of $C$ and to find the unique solution from the [[General Form]].
>[!Warning]
>Do not forget the [[Constant of Integration]], as we have taken an [[Indefinite Integral]] not a [[Definite Integral]].
