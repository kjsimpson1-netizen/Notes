One form of [[Ordinary Differential Equations|ODE]] that always works with [[Substitution]] are Bernoulli equations. This take the form:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
y'+p(x)y&= q(x)y^n
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
These look very similar to one we can use [[Integration Factor Method]] for except for the $y^n$. To solve these, we always make the [[Substitution]] of $v=y^{1-n}$.
