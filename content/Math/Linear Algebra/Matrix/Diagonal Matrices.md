This is any square [[Matrix]] that has all zero entries except for the diagonal row from the upper left corner down. These matrices have the property that they behave as a [[Scalar]] during [[Matrix Multiplication]]. 
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{bmatrix}
a&0&0\\
0&a&0\\
0&0&a
\end{bmatrix}\qquad a\in\mathbb{R}
\end{aligned}
$
$
