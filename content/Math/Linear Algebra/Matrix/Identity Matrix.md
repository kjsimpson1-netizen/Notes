This is any square [[Matrix]] that has a diagonal row of 1's across it (or a [[Diagonal Matrices]] with 1's). This will have the effect of having the [[Matrix Multiplication]] with it treating it as 1. For example, $I_3$ is the $3\times3$ identity matrix:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_3&= \begin{bmatrix}
1&0&0&\dots&0\\
0&1&0&\dots&0\\
0&0&1&\dots&0\\
\vdots&\vdots&\vdots&\ddots\\
0&0&0&&1
\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
