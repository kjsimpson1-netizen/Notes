These are found using the [[Outer Product]] of a [[Vector]] with itself, using the following equation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
P&= \frac{\vec v\otimes\vec v}{\vec v\cdot \vec v}
\end{aligned}
$$
Or, expressed in [[Dirac Notation]]:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P&= \frac{\ket {v}\bra v}{\bra v\ket v}
\end{aligned}
$$
## Properties
1. Projector [[Operators]] have the special characteristics that they will project any [[Vector]] multiplied by them onto itself:
   $$
\begin{aligned}
\hat P\ket b=c_1\ket v
\end{aligned}
$$
2. The sum of all the projectors of the full [[basis]] will give the [[Identity Matrix]]:
   $$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_jP_j=\mathbb{I}
\end{aligned}
$$
