This is the same as [[Lower Triangular Matrix]], however in the upper corner rather than the lower, taking the following form:
$$\begin{align*}
\begin{bmatrix}
*&*&*\\
0&*&*\\
0&0&*
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align*}$$
>[!Note]
>A unit upper triangle matrix is the same as shown above however with the diagonal all being equal to 1$$\begin{align*}
\begin{bmatrix}
1&*&*\\
0&1&*\\
0&0&1
\end{bmatrix}
\end{align*}$$
