This is the [[Matrix]] representation for a [[Linear System]]. It is represented by:
$$\begin{align*}
A\vec x&= \vec b
\end{align*}$$
Where $A$ is a square coefficient matrix of the coefficients of the variables and $\vec x$ is the variable vector, and $\vec b$ is the constant vector. We can then solve for the $\vec x$ by multiplying $\vec b$ by the [[Inverse Matrices]].
$$\begin{align*}
\vec x&= \bf{A}^{-1}\vec b
\end{align*}$$
