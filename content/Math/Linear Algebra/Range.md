The range of a $m\times n$ [[Matrix]] $A$ is given by:
$
\begin{aligned}
R(A)=\set{A\vec x:\vec x\in \mathbb R^n}
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Theorems]
>Letting $A$ be a $m\times n$ [[Matrix]], then the range $R(A)$ is a [[Subspace]] of $\mathbb R^m$. Also, $\dim(R(A))=\text{rank}(A)$.
