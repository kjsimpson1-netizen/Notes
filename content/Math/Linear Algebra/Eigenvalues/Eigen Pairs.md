For each square [[Matrix]] that is $n\times n$, there are $n$ eigen pairs. 
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Eigen Pair}=\{\lambda_i,\vec v_i\}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
However, if there is a repeated root, then the root has an algebraic multiplicity of the amount of times that it was repeated. This causes issues, as there must be $n$ unique vectors for a $n\times n$ matrix.
