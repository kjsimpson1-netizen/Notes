This is the double bars of [[Vector]], and it refers to the absolute value of the vector. This value is mathematically equivalent to the root of the summation of the squares of the components. For a vector that is an element of $\mathbb{R}^2$, this is the equivalent function:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec{c} &\in \mathbb{R}^n \\ ||\vec{c}|| &= \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}{c_i}^2}
\end{aligned}
$$
