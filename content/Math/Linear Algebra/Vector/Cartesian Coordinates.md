This is breaking up a [[Vector]] into it's $x$ and $y$ components. The x-component is represented with a $\hat{i}$, the y-component is $\hat{j}$, z-component is $\hat{k}$. For example, the vector $\vec{a}\in \mathbb{R}^2$ can be expressed as:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec a&= (a_1, a_2)=a_1(1,0), a_2(0,1)=a_1\hat i,a_2\hat j
\end{aligned}
$$
>[!Note]
>The letters are only used for the 2 and the 3 dimensonal spaces, anything else will use a [[Linea Combination]]
