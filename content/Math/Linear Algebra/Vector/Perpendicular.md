## 2 Dimensional
In order to make [[Vector]] that is are an element of $\mathbb{R}^2$ perpendicular to $\vec{a}$, we swap the location of the components and make one of the two negative, for example:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
&&\vec a&= (a_1,a_2)\\
&\therefore&\vec a_\perp&= (a_2, -a_1)\\
&&&\text{and}\\
&\therefore&\vec{a}_\perp&= (-a_2,a_1)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
This can be checked because the [[Dot Product]] of the perpendicular vector will be 0, indicating that it is perpendicular.
