This is one method to represent the direction of a [[Vector]] in 3 dimensional space. The direction angle uses three angles, $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ to define the vector's position in space. The $\alpha$ angle is with respect to the positive $x-axis$, the $\beta$ angle is with respect to the positive $y-axis$ and the $\gamma$ angle is with respect to the positive $z-axis$. These 3 values can be derived by the [[Cartesian Coordinates]] of the vector and the [[Euclidian Norm]] of the vector:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha=\arccos\left(\frac{\vec x}{||\vec v||}\right)&&\beta=\arccos\left(\frac{\vec y}{||\vec v||}\right)&&\gamma=\arccos\left(\frac{\vec z}{||\vec v||}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
>[!Note]
>The sum of the cosines squared of the three direction angles will always be 1. 

$$
\begin{aligned}
\cos^2\alpha+\cos^2\beta+\cos^2\gamma&= 1
\end{aligned}
$$
