This is similar to an [[Dot Product|Inner Product]] for two vectors $\vec a$ and $\vec b$ ($\vec a$ is a $m\times 1$ vector and $\vec b$ is a $n\times 1$ vector), however instead of the [[Vector|Vectors]] turning into a [[Scalar]] as in with the [[Dot Product]], this will turn into a [[Matrix]]. The outer product is given by:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\vec a\otimes\vec b=\begin{bmatrix}
a_1b_1&a_1b_2&\cdots&a_1b_n\\
a_2b_1&a_2b_2&\cdots&a_2b_n\\
\vdots&\vdots&\ddots&\vdots\\
a_nb_1&a_nb_2&\cdots&a_nb_n\\
\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
>[!Note]
>The [[Dot Product|Inner Product]] is also the [[Trace]] of the [[Outer Product]]
