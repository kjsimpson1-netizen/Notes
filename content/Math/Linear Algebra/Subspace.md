A subspace is a subset of a given [[Vector]] space. For example, if we have the space represented by $\bf{R}^3$, then if we have another [[Vector]] space that spans $\bf R^2$, we can say that the smaller one is a sub space of the larger one. This is analogous to saying that a plane is a subspace of a volume, and a curve is a subspace of a plane (provided that it is contained within the plane). This is represented by $\bf{R^2\subseteq R^3}$. 
>[!Formal Definition]
>A subset $U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^n$ is a **subspace** if:
>1. $U$ contains the zero vector, $\vec 0$
>2. $\Big[u_1+u_2\in U\Big]\forall u_1,u_2\in U$
>3. $\Big[c\vec u\in U\Big]\forall c\in \mathbb{R},\vec u\in U$
>Condition 2 is called closed under addition, and condition 3 is closed under scalar multiplication (condition 3 with $c=0\implies$ condition 1)
