This will occur when there are two [[Vector]]s that cannot be composed of each other. This can be found by comparing the coefficients of the entries, and if they are scalar multiples of each other then they are parallel:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec n_1&= s\vec n_2,&s\neq0\in \mathbb{R}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
If they are not parallel, then they have linear independence. This can also be done with the [[Determinant]] of the two vectors if they have equal rows and columns, if the [[Determinant]] is 0 then they have linear dependence. 
>[!Note]
>If there are more vectors than there are [[Planes]], then the vectors must have linear dependence
