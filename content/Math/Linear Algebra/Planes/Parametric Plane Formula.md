You can also use the [[Parametric Formula]] to express a plane:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boxed{\vec x=\vec p+s\vec l_1+t\vec l_2\,\,\,\,s,t\in \mathbb{R}}
\end{aligned}
$$
What this equation is saying is that $\vec x$ is equal to $\vec l_1$ and $\vec l_2$ extended indefinitely in any direction from $\vec p$.
>[!Warning]
>$\vec l_1$ and $\vec l_2$ must be non-parallel and must not have [[Linear Independence]]
