If in an [[Augmented Matrix]] $\vec b=0$, then the [[Augmented Matrix]] will be homogeneous. However, if there are any nonzero values, then it is non homogenous. Because of the [[Rank]] rules, the homogeneous matrices will always have at least one solution, and $\vec x=0$ will always be a solution. 
>[!Note]
>The general solution can always be written as the homogenous solution plus the particular solution
