Linear operators will change [[Vector]] into another [[Vector]]. They will be denoted by a hat on the letter. These are often [[Matrix|Matrices]] denoted by a hat, $\hat O$, and will take a vector in it's space and turn it into another vector. In order to understand how $\hat O$ will act upon all states, we only need to know how it acts upon the [[Eigenbasis]]. Since this is just a [[Linear Combination]], we therefore know how it will act upon any state.
