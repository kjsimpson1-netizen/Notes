An operator acts upon an object to undergo a predetermined operation. They are denoted by a letter with a hat, i.e. $\hat O$. 
## Vectors
Linear operators will change [[Vector]] into another [[Vector]]. In order to understand how $\hat O$ will act upon all states, we only need to know how it acts upon the [[Eigenbasis]]. Since this is just a [[Linear Combination]], we therefore know how it will act upon any state. Another thing to note is that when applied, they will change the [[Eigenvector]] directions. 
>[!Note]
>These are just a special kind of [[Linear Transformation]] where the inputs are mapped to the same space, $V\to V$

In order to get the inverse operator, the one that undoes what the regular one does, we just need to take the [[Adjoint]] of the [[Matrix]]
## Functions
These act upon a function and change it into another function. Some examples of these are the [[Derivative]] and [[Integral]], both of which map from one function to another.
