An adjoint is an operation  done on a [[Matrix]], where the [[Transposition]] of the [[Matrix]] is taken, then the [[Complex Conjugate]] of each entry is taken. This is represented by $A^\dagger$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
(A^T)^*=A^\dagger
\end{aligned}
$$

>[!Note]
>The adjoint of a product of two matrices is equal to:
>$$
\begin{aligned}
(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger
\end{aligned}
$$
