These are a special kind of [[Operator]] that will cause rotations. These [[Operator|Operators]] will be [[Hermitian Matrix]] and [[Unitary Matrices]] (provided they are normalized). A rotation operator takes the form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat R(\phi n)=e^{i\hat J_n}
\end{aligned}
$$With a [[Matrix Exponents]], Where $\hat{J}_n$ is the [[Generators|Generator]] of the rotation, and $n$ denotes the axial direction, around which the rotations will occur.  
>[!Warning]
>These [[Operator|Operators]] do not commute, i.e.:$$
\begin{aligned}
\hat{R}y\hat{R}_x\vec v\neq\hat{R}x\hat{R}_y\vec v
\end{aligned}
$$
