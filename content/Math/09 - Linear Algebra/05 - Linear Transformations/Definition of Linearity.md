A particular transformation is a [[Linear Transformation]] if the transformed version of the [[Linear Combination|Linear Combinations]] of two vectors is the same as the image of the two individual [[Vector]].
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boxed{T(\alpha \vec{x_1}+\beta\vec{x_1})= \alpha T(\vec {x_1})+ \beta T(\vec{x_2})}
\end{aligned}
$$
>[!Note]
>All [[Matrix Multiplication]] is linear
