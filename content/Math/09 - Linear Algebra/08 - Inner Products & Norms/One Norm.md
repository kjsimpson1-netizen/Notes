## [[Vector|Vectors]]
This is the sum of all of the entries of a [[Vector]] i.e.:
$$
\begin{aligned}
||\vec v||_1=\sum_{i}||v_i||
\end{aligned}
$$
## [[Matrix|Matrices]]
For a [[Matrix]], this is the maximum column sum value:
$$
\begin{aligned}
||A||_1=\max_{1\le j\le n}\sum_{i}^m||a_{ij}||
\end{aligned}
$$
