An inner product is an [[Operator]] that captures the length, angle, and orthogonality of two vectors. Any two vector spaces of the same size, i.e. $\vec x, \vec y\in\mathbb{R}^N$, can have a multitude of different inner products done on them, and as long as they have:
1. Linearity
2. Positive definiteness
Then they are defined as an inner product.
