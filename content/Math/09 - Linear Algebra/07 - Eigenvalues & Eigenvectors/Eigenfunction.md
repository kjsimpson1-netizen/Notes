An [[Eigenfunction]] is a function that when it has an [[Operator]] acted upon it, is a scaled version of itself (by the [[Eigenvalues|Eigenvalue]]):
$$
\begin{aligned}
L\Big[\phi(x)\Big]=\lambda \phi(x)
\end{aligned}
$$
An example of one of these is the function being the exponential, and the [[Operator]] being the derivative. Given $\phi(x)=e^{kx}$, and $L=\frac d{dx}$, the following is true:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac d{dx}\Big[e^{kx}\Big]&= ke^{kx}
\end{aligned}
$$
Here, $k$ is the [[Eigenvalues|Eigenvalue]] and $e^{kx}$ is the [[eigenfunction]].
