A set of [[Vector|vectors]]
$$
\{\vec u_1,\ldots,\vec u_m\}
$$
forms a **basis** of a [[Subspace|subspace]] $U$ if:
1. The vectors are **[[Linear Independence|linearly independent]]**.
2. The vectors **[[Span|span]]** $U$:
$$
\operatorname{span}\{\vec u_1,\ldots,\vec u_m\}=U.
$$
In other words, a basis is a set of vectors that:
* contains **no redundant vectors** (linear independence), and
* contains **enough vectors to generate the entire space** (spanning).
> [!Note]
> A basis gives us a set of vectors that can be used to represent **every vector in the space uniquely** as a linear combination.
