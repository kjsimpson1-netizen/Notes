A **linear relation** is a linear combination of [[Vector]]s that is equal to the zero vector:

$$
a_1\vec v_1+a_2\vec v_2+\cdots+a_n\vec v_n=\vec 0
$$

where $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_n$ are [[Scalar]]s called the **coefficients**.

> [!Note]
> A set of vectors is **linearly dependent** if there exists a linear relation where at least one coefficient is non-zero.
>
> If the only way to satisfy
>
> $$
> a_1\vec v_1+a_2\vec v_2+\cdots+a_n\vec v_n=\vec 0
> $$
>
> is for
>
> $$
> a_1=a_2=\cdots=a_n=0,
> $$
>
> then the vectors are [[Linear Independence|linearly independent]].
