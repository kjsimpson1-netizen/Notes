There are characterized by [[Horizontal Asymptotes]] on the graph, and this is the value the the function approaches as x goes to infinity.
$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow\pm\infty}f(x)&= HA
\end{aligned}
$
It can only be one of the [[Horizontal Asymptotes]] if the graph approaches the value, however it is allowed to cross it, permitted that it will approach the value at infinity but not reach it. It can be usually found by looking at the ratio of the [[Dominant Terms]] in the numerator and the denominator.
