These are rules to evaluate a [[Limit]] quicker than evaluating a massive block of algebra, because it can be broke up with the following laws.
## Constant Multiple 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow a}c\cdot f(x)&= c\cdot f(x)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
## Sum 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow a}(f(x)+g(x))&= \lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)+\lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
## Difference
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow a}(f(x)-g(x))&= \lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)-\lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
## Product
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow a}(f(x)\cdot g(x))&= \lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x) \cdot \lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
## Quotient
This only applies where $g(x)\neq 0$
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lim_{x\rightarrow a}\frac{f(x)}{ g(x)}&= \frac{\lim_{x\rightarrow a}f(x)}{\lim_{x\rightarrow a}g(x)}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
