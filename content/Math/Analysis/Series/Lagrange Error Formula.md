This is the formula that bounds the maximal potential error from a [[Taylor Polynomials]]. This is done by taking the fact that the size of subsequent terms in a [[Taylor Series]] that passes [[Convergence Tests]] will always decrease in magnitude, and therefore the largest potential error will be the $n+1$ term of the [[Taylor Polynomials]]. Therefore, the Lagrange error is given by:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
|E_n|\le\frac{f^{(n+1)}(c)}{(x+1)!}x^{(n+1)}
\end{aligned}
$
$
