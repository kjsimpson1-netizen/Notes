This is the interval on which a series will converge, and it can be found with the [[Radius of Convergence]]. For a given series, the interval of convergence will be:
 $
\begin{aligned}
x\in(a-r,a+r)
\end{aligned}
$Where $a$ is the center of the [[series]] and $r$ is the [[radius of convergence]].
>[!Note]
>The endpoints here are shown as round brackets, however these end points must be tested to determine whether or not they will converge there
