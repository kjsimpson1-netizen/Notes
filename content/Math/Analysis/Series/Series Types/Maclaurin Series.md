This is a simpler version of the [[Taylor Polynomials]] with $c=0$. The Taylor Series will become:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
f(x)&= \sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{f^{(k)}(0)}{k!}\cdot x^k
\end{aligned}
$
$
>[!Note]
>For multiplying 2 Maclaurin series, we can sum product of all the coefficients with the same or lesser degree, then this becomes the coefficient of the new series
