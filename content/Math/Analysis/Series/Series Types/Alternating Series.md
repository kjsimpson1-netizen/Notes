These are series that include a term that flips the sign of each odd or even term, i.e. $(-1)^n$. These series will converge when the rest of the [[Sequences]] is decreasing in magnitude and that series approaches 0 as $n\rightarrow\infty$. 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^n
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
>[!Note]
>If the series meets all the criteria above, then it converges. However, it may not converge absolutely if the absolute value of $a_n$ diverges. This is called _conditional convergence_
