This is a list of numbers, like a function evaluated for all [[Integers]] values. This is expressed by the notation:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\{a_n\}_{n=1}^\infty\qquad \{a_1,a_2,a_3...a_n\}\qquad\left\{a_n=f(n)\right\}_{n=1}^\infty
\end{aligned}
$
$
We can then use a sequence to make a [[Math/Analysis/Series/Series]] by taking a sum of a set amount of the terms by taking the [[Partial Sum]]. Every sequence must either diverge to infinity or converge to a single value, or it can oscillate between finite values and approach no one value. If the value it approaches is finite, then it is convergent and otherwise it is divergent. 
$
$
\begin{aligned}
A&= \lim_{n\rightarrow\infty}a_n
\end{aligned}
$
$
If there are two sequences that both approach a finite value, we are able to add, subtract, multiply by a constant and use division and multiplication of the two limits.
