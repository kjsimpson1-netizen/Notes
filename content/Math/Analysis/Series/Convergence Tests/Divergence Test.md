For [[Math/Analysis/Series/Series]] $A=\sum a_n$, if the [[Sequences]] $a_n$ tends to any value other than 0 as $n\rightarrow\infty$, then the [[Math/Analysis/Series/Series]] will diverge. 
>[!Note]
>If the series does not diverge using the divergence test, this does not necessarily mean that the series converges
