This is the test to see if a [[Geometric Series]] will converge or diverge. The main basis of a [[Math/Analysis/Series/Series]] converging or diverging is the ratio of growth between subsequent terms. If this term, $r$, is $\le1$, then the series will converge as each term will shrink in size. However, if it is larger then it will diverge. 
$$\begin{align*}
\sum |r|^n \begin{cases}
r\in(0,1),\text{Convergence}\\
r\in(1,\infty),\text{Divergence}
\end{cases}
\end{align*}$$
