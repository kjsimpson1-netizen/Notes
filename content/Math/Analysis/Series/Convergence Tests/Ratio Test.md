This is the test for whether or not a [[Geometric Series]] will converge or diverge. This can be done by taking the nth and the nth+1 terms of the [[Sequences]], and then taking the ratio of this as the [[Limit]] goes to infinity. This is similar to the [[Geometric Series Test]] and the [[Limit Comparison Test]], as if the ratio is less than one, then the series will converge, and if it is greater than one then the series will diverge, and the ratio being 1 tells us nothing and further [[Convergence Tests]] must be done. In order to see what happens at the boundary points, we must set $x$ equal to the boundary value, and see what happens using other [[Convergence Tests]].$$
