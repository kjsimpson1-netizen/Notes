These are the better versions of a [[Tangent Line]], and we will need [[Factorials]] to do this. We can express a Taylor polynomial using [[Partial Sum]], letting $a$ be a constant integer, and letting $n$ be a non-negative integer, where the $n^{th}$ degree Taylor polynomial is:
$
\begin{aligned}
f_n(x)&\approx \sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}\cdot(x-a)^k
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>For this to be a useful approximation, we should do this around a point, $a$, that is easily calculatable
