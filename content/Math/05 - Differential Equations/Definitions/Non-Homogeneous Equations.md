These occur when the equation doesn't equal 0, and therefore isn't [[Homogeneity|Homogenous]]. These take the form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
y''+ay'+b'=Cx+C...
\end{aligned}
$$
We can solve this first by treating the equation as a [[Homogeneity|Homogenous]] problem and therefore finding the general equation, and then adding the particular solution. We can also rewrite this as $y_c$ being the complimentary solution and $y_p$ being the particular solution, and therefore the final form is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
y&= y_c+y_p
\end{aligned}
$$
We can then solve for $y_p$ by using either [[Undetermined Coefficients]] method or by using [[Variation of Parameters]].
