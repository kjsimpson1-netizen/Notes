This is important for many identities. Let $u(x)$, $v(x)$, be sufficiently smooth. Then the following holds:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^l(v\mathcal{L}u-u\mathcal{L}v)dx&= -v(pu')\Big|_0^l+u(v'p)\Big|_0^l
\end{aligned}
$$
This can be shown by [[Integration by Parts]].
Suppose in addition that $w,v$ satisfy the [[Boundary Conditions]] of a[[Sturm-Liouville]]. We then  have that:$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^lv\mathcal{L}udx&= \int_0^lu\mathcal{L}vdx
\end{aligned}
$$
