These are the roots of a number. From the theorem of algebra, a nth degree polynomial will have n roots, and the equation for these roots of unit are:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega_n:&= e^{\frac{2\pi i}n}\\
\omega_n&= \{1,\omega_n^2,...,\omega_n^{n-1}\}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is often used to solve for the repeated roots of a solution to a equation of [[Complex Numbers]].
