There are some functions that can be rewritten in terms of elementary functions:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\sin(z)&= \frac1{2i}(e^{iz}-e^{-iz})\\
\cos(z)&= \frac12(e^{iz}+e^{-iz}) 
\end{aligned}$$
$$
We also have elementary equations for hyperbolics:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\sinh(z)&= \frac{e^z-e^{-z}}2\\
\cosh(z)&= \frac{e^z+e^{-z}}2
\end{aligned}$$
$$
Some useful identities from this are:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\sin(z)&= \cosh(y)\sin(x)+i\cos(x)\sinh(y)\\
\cos(z)&= \cos(x)\cosh(y)-i\sin(x)\sinh(y)
\end{aligned}$$
$$
