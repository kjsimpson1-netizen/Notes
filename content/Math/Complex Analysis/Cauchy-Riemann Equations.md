These are are equations that determine whether or not a function is [[Analytic]] or not. The Cauchy-Riemann equations are:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
u_x=v_y\\
u_y=-v_x
\end{aligned}
$
$
>[!Note]
>We can also show that the following equality holds, and if it does then the function itself is analytic:
>$
$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial f(z)}{\partial \bar z}&= \frac12\left(\frac{\partial}{\partial x}+i\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\right)f(x)=0
\end{aligned}
$
$
