This is the rule used to differentiate [[Integral]] with a variable as the upper or lower bounds. This can be done with the following equation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac d{dx}\int_{a(x)}^{b(x)}f(t,x)dt&= f(b(x), x) \cdot \frac{d}{dx} b(x) - f(a(x), x) \cdot \frac{d}{dx} a(x) + \int_{a(x)}^{b(x)} \frac{\partial}{\partial x} f(t, x) \, dt
\end{aligned}
$$
