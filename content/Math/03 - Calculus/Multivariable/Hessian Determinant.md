 This is the method to determine whether or not a [[Critical Points]] in N dimensional will be a local minima, maxima or a saddle point, or none of the above. The hessian[[Determinant]]] of the [[Hessian Matrix]] in 2 dimensions takes the form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
D(x,y)&= f_{xx}f_{yy}-f_{xy}^2
\end{aligned}
$$
>[!Note]
>The following will tell us the nature of the critical point:
>- If $D>0$ and $f_{xx}>0$ then it is a local minimum
>- If $D>0$ and $f_{xx}<0$ then it is a local maximum
>- If $D<0$, then it is a saddle point
>- If $D=0$, then the test is inconclusive
