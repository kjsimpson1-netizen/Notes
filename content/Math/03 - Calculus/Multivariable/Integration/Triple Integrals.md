This is the same idea as a [[Double Integrals|Double Integral]], however with 3 dimensions, and it takes the form:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\iiint_{E}f(x,y,z)dVol\qquad E=\Big\{(x,y,z), a\le x\le b, c\le y \le d, e\le z\le f\Big\}
\end{aligned}
$$
This can be thought of as a volume (3 dimensional). Some examples of this are density

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} \, dx = \frac{x}{2} \sqrt{a^2 - x^2} + \frac{a^2}{2} \sin^{-1}\left(\frac{x}{a}\right) + C
\end{aligned}
$$
