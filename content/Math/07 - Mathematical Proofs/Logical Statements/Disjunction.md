This is done using the word "or". The disjunction of two [[Logical Statements]] ($P$ and $Q$) can be shown with $P\lor Q$. This is true when either $P$ or $Q$ is true. The [[Truth Tables]] for this is:

| $P$ | $Q$ | $P\lor Q$ |
| --- | --- | --------- |
| T   | T   | T         |
| T   | F   | T         |
| F   | T   | T         |
| F   | F   | F         |
>[!Note]
>Mathematical "or" is inclusive i.e. at least one of the two statements is true. Spoken english (called $\bf\text{xor}$) and this is exclusive such that exactly one of the statements is true. This gives the statement:
>

| $P$ | $Q$ | $P\lor Q$ |
| --- | --- | --------- |
| T   | T   | F         |
| T   | F   | T         |
| F   | T   | T         |
| F   | F   | F         |
