This is the transfer of [[Heat]] through electromagnetic waves such as light. Every object emits [[Electromagnetic Radiation]] through [[Blackbody Radiation]]. The rate of energy radiation can be represented with the following equation:
### Stefan-Boltzmann's Law
$$\begin{align*}
H&= Ae\sigma T^4\\
\\
H&= \text{Heat}\\
A&= \text{Area}\\
e&= \text{Emissivity}\\
\sigma &= \text{Stefan-Boltzmann constant}\\
T&= \text{Temperature (Kelvin)}
\end{align*}$$
The Stefan-Boltzmann constant gets larger with darker surfaces and smaller with lighter ones. This can also be shown with [[Albedo]].
## Net Heat Flow
While it is true that the object is radiating [[Heat]], the environment around the object is also emitting heat, which is absorbed by the object. If it is in [[Thermal Equilibrium]] with it's surroundings, then the heat flowing out from the surface due to radiation must be equal to the heat flowing in from it's surroundings. 
$$\begin{align*}
H_\text{net}&= H_\text{emit}-H_\text{absorbed}\\
&= Ae\sigma T^4- Ae\sigma {T_s}^4\\
&= Ae\sigma (T^4-{T_s}^4)
\end{align*}$$
For this equation, a positive $H$ indicates a heat flow out of the object, a negative indicates a heat flow in.
>[!Note]
>If we are talking about object orbiting a star absorbing radiation, then we use the area of a circle for the area of radiation that is absorbed, as this is what the star "sees"

## Intensity
This is the amount of energy that reaches a certain square unit of area, represented by the equation:
$$\begin{align*}
I&= \frac HA
\end{align*}$$
The units for this are $\frac W{m^2}$.
