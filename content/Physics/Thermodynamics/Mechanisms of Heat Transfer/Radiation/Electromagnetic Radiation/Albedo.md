This is the percentage of light that is reflected by an object, therefore it must be a number between 0 and 1. This is useful because if it has an albedo of 1, this means that absorbs no [[Radiation|Radiation]], and if it has an albedo of 1, then it absorbs all of the [[Radiation]] that hits it. We albedo is mentioned in a question, we multiply the entire equation for radiation by $(1-\alpha)$ to account of the albedo.
