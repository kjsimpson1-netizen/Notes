James Clerk Maxwell was the scientist that found that light is nothing but a combination of electric field and magnetic field. The oscillating electric field causes an oscillating magnetic field, and this loop continues perpetually. He also found that the speed of light is related to electric permissively and magnetic permeability. Shorter wavelengths and higher frequencies have a more violet colour, and longer wavelengths will lower frequencies have a more red colour. The higher the frequency and the lower the wavelength of light, the higher the energy. [[Blackbody Radiation]] usually occurs and longer wavelengths with lower frequencies, as this has lower energy. The higher the temperature gets, the more energy it carries and therefore it shifts through visible light, from red to white.
