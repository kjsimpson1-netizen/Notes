This is how much [[Radiation]] that will come from a specific material, on a scale form 0 to 1. The the emissivity is equal to 0, then it is a perfect reflector and will absorb none of the energy through [[Radiation]]. This is why things such as shiny aluminium is used as the material for thermoses. If it is 1, then it is a blackbody and will receive and emit 100% of the energy from [[Radiation]]. The emissivity of an object is found by comparing it to a blackbody with the following equation:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
e&= \frac{H_\text{object}}{H_\text{blackbody}}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
