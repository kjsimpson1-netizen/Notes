This is the relationship between the wavelength of the light and the amount of [[Radiation]] it emits.

The precise relationship between these two is:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\lambda_\text{max}&= \frac{2.9\times10^{-3}\;K\cdot mm}{T}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
If we take the area under this curve between 2 bounds, we can say that it is equal to the fraction of the power in that certain wavelength range between those two bounds. 
By looking at this curve, we can see that the light emitted through [[Blackbody Radiation]] has a variety of different wavelengths, and therefore a variety of colours. This also means that objects with lower temperatures will emit energy that is not useful, mainly infrared. 
>[!Note]
>Just because our eyes tell us that there is a peak colour present due to [[Blackbody Radiation]], this does not mean that it is the maximum wavelength, as our eyes cannot see all wavelengths.
