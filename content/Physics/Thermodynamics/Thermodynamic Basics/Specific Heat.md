Every object has it's own specific amount of [[Heat]] energy required to raise it's temperature 1$\degree$C per kg of mass, and this specific value is known as specific heat. Some objects have higher specific heats because the [[Heat]] energy is transferred to vibrations and rotations of the individual molecules rather than bouncing off the other molecules, which we perceive as temperature.
$
\begin{aligned}
Q&= mc\Delta T
\end{aligned}
$
This is the equation for the amount of [[Heat]] (Q) required to raise the temperature of a material (specific heat of $c$) by 1$\degree$C per kilogram of mass.
