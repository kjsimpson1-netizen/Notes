## Thermal Expansion
This is the idea that materials expand when [[Heat]] is applied. This occurs because the average distance between the molecules will increase as the molecular potential energy of these molecules increase, with these molecules having a tendency to want to increase the distance rather than decreasing. This will cause overall length or volume of the object to increase. 
### Linear Expansion
If the change in temperature, or $\Delta$T is not too large (100$\degree$C or less or not causing a phase change), then the change in length, or $\Delta$L is linear and can be defined by the equation:
$$\Delta L= \alpha L_0\Delta T$$
With $\alpha$ representing the coefficient of linear expansion specific to each material. 
>[!Warning]
>There is a common misconception that a hole in a sheet of metal will shrink as it is heated to the sheet expanding, however this is not true as the entire sheet expands and therefore the hole also gets larger.
