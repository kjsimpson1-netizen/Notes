The thermodynamic processes that occur in nature are irreversible, and are processes that can proceed spontaneously in one direction, but not the other. Examples of this are waste heat from friction, heat flowing into a cold object, etc. This is an example of the [[2nd Law of Thermodynamics]]. Any [[Heat Engine]] is an example of an irreversible process, as the energy that changes from the potential [[Heat]] energy from the hot and could reservoirs that turns into energy is not reversible.
