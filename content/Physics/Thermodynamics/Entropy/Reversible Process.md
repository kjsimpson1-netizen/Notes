Reversible processes are not free of the direction of the process, however it is on the cusp of being able to flow in reverse, so an infinitesimally small change of either part of the system would cause the process to go in reverse.
We can also call reversible processes equilibrium processes, as we are so infinitesimally close to the equilibrium point as to make the process reversible.
>[!Note]
>These are very idealised processes, and they are very hard to replicate in real life, rather just a useful concept to think about
