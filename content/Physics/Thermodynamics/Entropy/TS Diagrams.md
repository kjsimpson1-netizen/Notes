These are diagrams with the axis of temperature and [[Physics/Thermodynamics/Entropy/Entropy]]. We can interpret the area underneath the lines as the quantity of [[Heat]] that flows into or out of the system, and the area inside the curves is the total work that the system does. The horizontal lines are [[Isothermal]], whereas the vertical lines are **[[Isentropic]]**. The only processes that can be plotted on these axes are those that are [[Reversible Process]].
Much like calculating [[Work]] from [[Pressure Volume Processes]], we can again use the area enclosed in the shape. The [[Heat]] is the area underneath the part of the cycle where the [[Heat]] flows in.
