This is the idea that the pressure of a gas is directly proportional to the volume of the gas, the temperature of the gas and how much gas (in moles or grams) are contained in the container. We can relate this with the following equation:
$$\begin{align*}
PV&= nRT\\
\\
P&= \text{Pressure}\\
V&= \text{Volume}\\
n&= \text{Moles}\\
R&= \text{Gas Constant}\\
T&= \text{Temperature}
\end{align*}$$
