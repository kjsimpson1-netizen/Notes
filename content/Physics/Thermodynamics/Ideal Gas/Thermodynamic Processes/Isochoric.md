These are [[Pressure Volume Processes]] in which the volume is held constant. 
Since this is true, we can rearrange the equation from the [[1st Law of Thermodynamics]]:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta U&= Q\cancel{-W}&\textcolor{red}{\text{W=0}}\\
\Delta U&= Q\\
nC_VT&= Q
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
In this form of [[Pressure Volume Processes]], all the heat that is added is converted to internal energy and vice vera.
