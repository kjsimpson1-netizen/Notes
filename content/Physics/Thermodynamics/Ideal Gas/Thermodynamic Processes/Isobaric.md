This is [[Pressure Volume Processes]] in which the pressure is maintained at a constant value. 
## Work
Although none of the variables in the [[1st Law of Thermodynamics]] are zero, it does make the [[Work]] easy to calculate with the following equation:
$
\begin{aligned}
W&= p\Delta V
\end{aligned}
$
## Heat
We can also express this this to determine the $Q$ of the processes:
$
\begin{aligned}
Q&= nC_P\Delta T
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>We can also find the $C_P$ with $C_V$ with the following equation:
>$
\begin{aligned}
C_V&= C_P+R
\end{aligned}
$

## Internal Energy
The internal energy in this type of thermodynamic process is expressed by the following:
$
\begin{aligned}
\Delta U&= nC_PT-P\Delta V
\end{aligned}
$
