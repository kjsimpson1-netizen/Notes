This is a thermodynamic processes in which the temperature of the system is maintained. Although like [[Isobaric|Isobaric]] systems, there are no zero values, we can assume that all the change in [[Internal Energy]] is 0, and therefore the equation from the [[1st Law of Thermodynamics]] is:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
Q&= W
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
>[!Note]
>This assumes that there is an ideal gas in the system, if the gas is non ideal, the temperature may stay the same but the [[Internal Energy]] may change

## Finding Work
We can express the work done by a system as the following:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
P(V)&= \frac{nRT}V\\
W&= \int_{V_1}^{V_2}\ln(V)\;dV
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
These graphs are reciprocals, with the further than distance between the origin and the curve, the greater the temperature
