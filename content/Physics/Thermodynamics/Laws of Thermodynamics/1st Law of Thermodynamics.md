This is the [[Conservation of Energy]] with thermodynamics. This relates the [[Heat]] and the [[Work]] to the internal energy of the gas. 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\\
&\text{Physics}\\
\Delta U&= Q-W\\
\hline\\
&\text{Chemistry}\\
\Delta U&= Q_{rev}+Q_{irrev}+W_{rev}+W_{irrev{}}\\
\hline\\
Q:&\text{Heat}\\
\Delta U:&\text{Internal Energy}\\
W:&\text{Work done by gas}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
>[!Note]
>The $\Delta U$ is completely independent of the path that it goes on to get to that state.
>The sign for the work is different between physics and chemistry, because in physics we care about the work done by the gas, and in chem we care about the work done on a gas
