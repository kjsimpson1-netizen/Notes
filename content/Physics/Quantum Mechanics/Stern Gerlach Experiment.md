This experiment conducted gives a stream of silver atoms. Silver was chosen because it is electrically and chemical neutral, and it has one free valence electron. This allows the experiment to measure the magnetic moment of a singular effective electron.
The electron stream was shot between a series of magnets that created a magnetic gradient field in the z direction. 
### Results
Instead of a continuous line of silver, there were only two discrete states. This implied the idea of electron spin being only up or down, and that the orbital angular momentum was quantized, and not continuous. 
Let $\ket{\psi}$ describe the state of one of the silver atoms in the Stern-Gerlach experiment, and $\ket{+z}$ vector represent the state with $S_z=+\frac{\bar{h}}2$. We can represent these states using [[Dirac Notation]], or:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\braket{+z|+z}|^2&= 1\\
|\braket{-z|+z}|^2&= 0
\end{aligned}
$$
Translated into English, the probability of the spin up being in a spin up state is 1, and the probability of a spin down being in a spin up state is 0.
## Thought Experiment
Now, instead of measure the two discrete points on the output, we will only select one of the two silver streams (either spin up or spin down), blocking the other one. 
### Experiment 1
In this experiment, we take $N_0$ silver atoms and send them through the SGz. After the SGz, the spin down silver atoms are blocked, and the spin up are allowed to go through into an SGx. After this, it is measured that $\frac14N_0$ are positive in the $x$ direction, and $\frac14N_0$ of them are spin down in the $x$ direction. 
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\braket{+z|+x}|^2&= \frac{1}{2}\\
|\braket{-z|+x}|^2&= \frac{1}{2}\\\hline
|\braket{+z|-x}|^2&= \frac{1}{2}\\
|\braket{-z|-x}|^2&= \frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
### Experiment 2
 Experiment 1 is repeated, however now out of the SGx, we send it through another SGz. However contrary to what we may expect with all of the SGz being spin up, as this direction was already measured and the spin down was blocked, we instead see that again $\frac12$ of the silver atoms have spin up, and the other $\frac12$ have spin down, even though we have already blocked all of the spin down previously. 
### Experiment 3
Now instead of filtering out the spin down SGx, we send both through the second SGz, we now see that everything is spin up again out of the second SGz. 
## Outcome
This shows that the measurement of the SGx somehow changed the measured quantity of the SGz measurement. When blocked, the SGx is somehow interfering with the measurement of SGz.
