In the [[Stern Gerlach Experiment]], the electron of the lone silver atom is found to have a spin 1/2 state of either up or down. Since we know that the electron is either spin up or down, we have to have that $\braket{-z|+z}=0$ (along with all of the other directions). In order to have this, we have:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\ket{+z}=\begin{pmatrix}
1\\0
\end{pmatrix}&&\ket{-z}=\begin{pmatrix}
0\\1
\end{pmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$
Since we also have that after the SGx, the electrons then have a 50/50 chance of being spin up or spin down, we have:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\braket{+x|+z}=\frac{1}{2}&&\braket{+x|-z}=\frac{1}{2}
\end{aligned}
$$
