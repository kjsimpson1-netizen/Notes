These are [[Matrix|Matrices]] that are very important to quantum mechanics. The 4 Pauli matrices are:
$$
\begin{array}{cc}
I = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} & X = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \\
\\
Y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix} & Z = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}
\end{array}
$$
These [[Matrix|Matrices]] are also [[Unitary Matrices]] and [[Hermitian Matrix]].
