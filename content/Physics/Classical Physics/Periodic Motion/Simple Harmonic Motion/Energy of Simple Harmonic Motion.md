Over the span of a cycle, the change in energy of [[Simple Harmonic Motion]] is 0, as it is assumed to be perfectly conserved, following the [[Conservation of Energy]]. We can express this as the following:
$$\begin{align*}
E&= \frac12m{V_x}^2+\frac12kx^2
\end{align*}$$
Where the energy of any two points is the exact same. We can also express the total energy with the following:
$$\begin{align*}
E_\text{max}&= \frac12A^2
\end{align*}$$
We can also express the maximum [[Kinetic Energy]] as:
$$\begin{align*}
KE_\text{max}&= \frac12m\omega^2A^2
\end{align*}$$
