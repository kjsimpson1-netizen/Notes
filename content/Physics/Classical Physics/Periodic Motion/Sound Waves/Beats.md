This is similar to the idea of [[Interference]], where multiple waves of the same [[Frequency]] but different [[Amplitude]] interact. However, with beats, the waves have the same [[Amplitude]] but different [[Frequency]]. This occurs when two tuning forks with slightly different frequencies are struck at the same time. 
This causes the two waves to periodically change from being in phase when the two waves align, to being out of phase when the two waves are half a wavelength out of phase. This causes pulsing in the sound as the wave goes from in phase to out of phase.
```desmos-graph
y=2.5\sin(10x)+2.5\sin(9x)
```
Each one of these groupings of sound waves are one beat, and then frequency of one beat is equal to the difference of the two different frequencies of the waves.
$$\begin{align*}
f_\text{beat}&= f_\text{a}-f_\text b\\
\hline\\
f&= \text{Frequency}
\end{align*}$$
