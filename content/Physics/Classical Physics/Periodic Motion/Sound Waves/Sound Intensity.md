Just like [[Wave Intensity]], this is the intensity of [[Sound Waves]]. We can express the wave intensity of a sound wave using one of the following equations:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I&= \frac12\sqrt{\rho B}\omega^2A^2\\
I&= \frac{{P_\text{max}}^2}{2\rho v}\\
I&= \frac{{P_\text{max}}^2}{2\sqrt{\rho B}}\\
\hline\\
I&= \text{Intensity}\\
\rho&= \text{Density}\\
B&= \text{Bulk Modulus}\\
\omega&= \text{Angular Frequency}\\
A&= \text{Displacement Amplitude}\\
v&= \text{Velocity}\\
P_\text{max}&= \text{Pressure Amplitude}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
>[!Note]
>The intensity as it moves away from the source is proportional to $\frac1{r^2}$
