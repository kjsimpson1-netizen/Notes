To measure the intensity of the sounds we hear, we often use logarithms, as there is a very broad range that we hear over. The equation for the [[Sound Intensity]] level is:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\beta&= (10\;dB)\log_{10}\left(\frac I{I_0}\right)\\
\hline\\
\beta&= \text{Sound Intensity Level}\\
I&= \text{Sound Wave Intensity}\\
I_0&= \text{Reference Intensity}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
The units for sound intensity level are decibels, or $\frac1{10}$ of a bel.
