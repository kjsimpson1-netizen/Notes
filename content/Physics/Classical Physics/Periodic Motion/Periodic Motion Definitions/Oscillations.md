This is when there is an [[Equilibrium Position]], where the force from either gravity or the force from the spring is equal to 0. However, instead of staying stationary at this position, it will overshoot it due to the object's kinetic energy. Then when the kinetic energy is completely depleted due to the normalizing force, called a **restoring force**, the object will reverse it's motion and attempt to return to the equilibrium position.
>[!Note]
>In order for this to happen, the **restoring force** must return the object to the equilibrium position
