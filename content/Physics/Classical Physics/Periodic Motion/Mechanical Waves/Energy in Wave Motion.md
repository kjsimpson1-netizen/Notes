All [[Mechanical Waves]] have an amount of energy that they carry with them, this is why ocean waves and light can both be so destructive. We can express the equation for the average energy carried by a wave as the following:
$
\begin{aligned}
P_\text{av}&= \frac12\sqrt{\mu F}\omega^2 A^2\\
\hline \\
F&= \text{Force}\\
\mu&= \text{Density per Unit Length}\\
A&= \text{Amplitude}\\
\omega &= \text{Angular Frequency}
\end{aligned}
$
