This is when there are multiple waves that are undergoing [[Interference]] at the exact same point. We can do this by taking the summation of all of the waves that are present at the location of the superposition, and the sum of these waves will be the [[Amplitude]] of the final wave at that point. We can express this algebraically as:
$$
\begin{aligned}
y(x,t)&= \sum\limits_{i=0}^ny_n(x,t)
\end{aligned}
$$
>[!Warning]
>This will only hold true for mediums that obey [[Young's Modulus]], as if they don't the quantity of the final wave will not be additive of the rest.
