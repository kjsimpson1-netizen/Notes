This is like the [[Energy in Wave Motion]] but rather than having only 1 dimension, this is for waves that move in 3 dimensions, such as light and sounds. For these, the equation for their intensity is:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
I&= \frac P{4\pi r^2}\\
\hline\\
I&= \text{Intensity}\\
P&= \text{Power}\\
r&= \text{Radius}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
