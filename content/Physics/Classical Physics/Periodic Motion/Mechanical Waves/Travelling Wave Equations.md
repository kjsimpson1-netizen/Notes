We can describe [[Mechanical Waves]] as a function of two variables, both the x-position and the time. The equation for them is:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
y(x,t)&=\begin{cases}
A\cos\Big[\omega\Big(\frac xv-t\Big)\Big]\\
A\cos\Big[2\pi\Big(\frac x\lambda-\frac tT\Big)\Big]
\end{cases}\\
\hline\\
\lambda &= \text{Wavelength}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
If we define the quantity $\frac{2\pi}\lambda$ as $k$, we can simplify the equation into:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
y(x,t)&= A\cos(kx-\omega t)
\end{aligned}$$
$$
