This an example of conservation of energy. It was first understood by the physicist Emmy Noether. She proved "Noether's Theorem" explains how conserved quantities are related to symmetries in nature. There is a certain amount of energy that can be assigned to any part a system, and the total energy of an isolated system doesn't change with time. This means that the energy can be transferred between the parts to different forms, but it will never be created or destroyed (the total energy of the isolated system remains the same).
