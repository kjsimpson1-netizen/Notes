An object at rest remains at rest, or if in motion, remains in motion at a constant velocity unless acted on by a net external [[Force]]. 
$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F_R&= 0\rightarrow\vec v=\text{conts.}
\end{aligned}
$
This sets the inertial reference frame for [[Newton's Second Law]]. If the first law holds, then the second one does as well.
