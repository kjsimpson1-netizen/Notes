This is the energy that is due to interactions between matter that is yet to be realized. 
Generally, potential energy is a negative number. This is because at infinity, the potential energy must be 0, as the potential energy is inversely proportional to the radius. It is negative, because intuitively the potential energy must decrease as the two objects get closer together, and in order for both of these to be true, the value must be negative. 
Another way to look at this is with [[Conservation of Energy]]:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
E_p+W&=0
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
In order for this to be true, one of these two must be negative, and since positive [[Work]] is put in, the potential energy must be 0.
