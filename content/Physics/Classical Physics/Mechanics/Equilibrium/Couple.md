This is when there are 2 [[Force]]s that are acting upon a single object with equal magnitudes but opposite directions (think about turning a wheel from the top and the bottom). The [[Torque]] of a couple is given by:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\boxed{\vec M_T= (r_1-r_2)\times\vec F}
\end{aligned}
$$
>[!Note]
>The torque is independent of the reference point of the [[Torque]]
