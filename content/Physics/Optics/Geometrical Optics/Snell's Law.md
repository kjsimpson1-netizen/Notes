This is the law that predicts how light will bend based off of the [[Index of Refraction]]. Each object has it's own index of refraction. 
This law states that if you multiply the index of refraction by the $\sin$ of the angle (from the normal line)
$
\begin{aligned}
n_1\sin\theta_1&= n_2\sin\theta_2
\end{aligned}
$
(Some of the light does get reflected at the same angle, on the other side of the normal line)
If the $\sin\theta$ is greater than 1, then there will be a total internal reflection, and all of the light will be internally reflected at the angle on the other side of the normal line
## Critical Angle
This is the angle of which all of the light will be internally reflected. This is when the angle on the slow side is $90\degree$.]
