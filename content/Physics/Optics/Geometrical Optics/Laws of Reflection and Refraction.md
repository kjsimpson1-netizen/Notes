1. The incident, reflected and refracted rays and the normal to the surface all lie in the same plane
2. The angle of refraction is equal to the angle of incidence
$$
   $$\begin{aligned}
\theta_r&= \theta_a
\end{aligned}$$
$$
3. The ratio of the sines of the angles of refraction and incidence is equal to the inverse ratio of the two (also known as [[Snell's Law]])
