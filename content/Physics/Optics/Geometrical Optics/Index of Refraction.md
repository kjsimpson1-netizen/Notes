This is the ratio of the speeds of light in a medium compared to that of the speed of light in a vacuum. 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
&n= \frac cv\\
\hline\\
n&= \text{Refractive Index}\\
c&= \text{Speed of Light}\\
v&= \text{Speed of Light In the material}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
>[!Note]
>The speed of light in any medium other than a 100% vacuum is greater than 1

## Wavelength
Whenever light enters a medium with a larger index of refraction, the light will slow down, and the wavelength will also get "squished" following this equation. However, the frequency of the light must remain unchanged. 
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\lambda&= \frac{\lambda_0}n\\
\hline\\
\lambda&= \text{Wavelength}\\
n&= \text{Index of Refraction}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
