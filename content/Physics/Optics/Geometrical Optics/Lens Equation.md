This is the equation that relates the [[Focal Length]] of a lens to it's object distance and it's image distance. The lens equation, also known as the lens maker's equation, is:
$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1f&= \frac1{d_o}+\frac1{d_i}\\
\hline\\
f&= \text{Focal Length}\\
d_o&= \text{Object Distance}\\
d_i&= \text{Image Distance}
\end{aligned}
$
