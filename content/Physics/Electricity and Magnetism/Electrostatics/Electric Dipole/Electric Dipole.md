This is when there are a pair of [[Electric Charge]] with equal and opposite charge separated by a distance. A good example of this are water molecules, since they have a net 0 charge, however the electrical dipole allows them to break apart solutes. 
## Uniform Field
If there is a uniform [[Electric Field]], then there will be no net force on the dipole as it the negative will cancel the positive, however there will still be a torque trying to align it with the electrical field. 
## Non-uniform field
If there is a non-uniform field, then there will also be a force as well as a torque.
