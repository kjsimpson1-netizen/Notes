Whenever an [[Electric Dipole]] changes direction in an [[Electric Field]], the [[Electric Field]] does work upon it, with a corresponding change in potential energy. This potential energy can be represented by:
$$\begin{align*}
U&= -\vec p\cdot\vec E\\
\hline\\
U&= \text{Potential Energy}\\
p&= \text{Dipole Moment}\\
E&= \text{Electric Field}
\end{align*}$$
This tells us that the potential energy is the most negative whenever the moment is parallel to the field, giving us a dot product of 0, and the most stable orientation.
