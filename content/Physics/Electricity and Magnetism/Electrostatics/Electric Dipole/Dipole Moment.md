This is the vector quantity that describes the direction and the strength of a given [[Electric Dipole]]. This can be found using the equation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
|\vec p|&= q\vec d\\
\hline\\
\vec p&= \text{Dipole Moment}\\
q&= \text{Charge}\\
\vec d&= \text{Distance}
\end{aligned}
$$
>[!Note]
>The distance vector here is defined to be going from the negative [[Electric Charge]] to the positive [[Electric Charge]]

We can also express this as:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec p=\int_{V}\rho(\vec r')\vec r'd\tau'
\end{aligned}
$$
