These are any objects that will easily conduct electricity and [[Electric Charge]] through them. These are often metals, such as copper in copper wires. 
>[!Note]
>The [[Electric Field]] inside of a conductor are 0, all the excess charge will rest on the surface of the conductor. This still applies if there is a charge inside of the conductor, as there will be an induced opposite charge inside of the conductor to ensure the net charge is 0 inside the conductor following [[Gauss's Law for Electricity]]

This is the principle that a Faraday's cage operates upon, as there must be no net [[Electric Field]] on the inside of a [[Conductor]], therefore the even a cavity on the inside is protected from [[Electric Charge]] on the outside.
