This is the Coulomb force between objects with [[Electric Charge]]. 
These are in reference to point charges with no true radius. These can either be attractive or repulsive forces. There can also be many charges acting on single point charge, and the forces on the single point mass must be summed. 
$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F&= \frac{k|q_1q_2|}{r^2}\hat r\\
\hline \\
\vec F&= \text{Force}\\
q&= \text{Charge}\\
r&= \text{radius}\\
k&= 9.00\times10^{9}\\
\hat r&= \text{Unit Vector}
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>If there are many charges acting upon each other, the total force is equal to the vector sum of all the individual forces, or the superposition of the forces
