Using the fact that the [[Electric Potential]] is related to the [[Electric Field]] through the following equation:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
V_b-V_a&= \int_a^b\vec E\cdot d\vec l\\
\hline\\
V&= \text{Voltage}\\
E&= \text{Electric Field}\\
l&= \text{Displacement}
\end{aligned}
$
$
We can then use this to calculate the electric field at any given point using the partial derivatives of the components of $x,y,z$.
