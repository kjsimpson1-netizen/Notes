This is the "flow" of [[Electric Field]] through a given point following [[Gauss's Law for Electricity]], with units of voltmeters ($V\cdot m$). The electric flux through a given area is:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\Phi_E&= \iint_S\vec E\cdot d\vec A\\
\hline\\
\Phi_E&= \text{Electric Flux}\\
\vec E&= \text{Electric Field}\\
A&= \text{Area}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
