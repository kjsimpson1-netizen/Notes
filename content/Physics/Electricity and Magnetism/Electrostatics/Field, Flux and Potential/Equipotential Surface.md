This is the same idea as [[Electric Field]] lines with [[Electric Potential]], which can help us visualize the [[Electric Potential]] at various points. These are drawn very similarly to topographical maps. 
>[!Note]
>Equipotential means that the [[Electric Potential]] is the same at every point.

Since the [[Electric Potential]] is the same at every point on the equipotential surface, we can say that the field lines and the equipotential surface is always mutually perpendicular. 
## Conductors
Whenever all [[Electric Charge]] is at rest on a [[Conductor]], the surface of the conductor is always an equipotential surface with all the entire volume of the conductor having the same potential.
