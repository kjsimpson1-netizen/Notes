 This is a derivation of the original [[Poisson's Equation]] that relates the [[Laplacian]] of the [[Electric Potential]] to the volumetric charge density through the following equation:
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla^2 \varphi&= -\frac\rho{\epsilon_0}\\
\hline\\
\varphi&= \text{Electric Potential}\\
\rho&= \text{Charge Density}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
In order to invert this equation, we can use the following:$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
V(r)=\frac1{4\pi R^2}\oint_{S_R}V(r')da'
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
>[!Note]
>If the charge density is 0 in the given space, then we can use the [[Laplace Equation]]
