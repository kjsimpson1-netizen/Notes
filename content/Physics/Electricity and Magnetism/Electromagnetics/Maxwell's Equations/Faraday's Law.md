This is the law that relates the induction of [[Current]] and [[Voltage]] through a changing [[Magnetic Flux]].
$
$
\begin{aligned}
\oint \vec E\cdot d \vec l&= \mathcal{E}= \left|\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}\right|\\
\hline\\
\vec E&= \text{Electric Field}\\
\vec l&= \text{Length}\\
\mathcal{E}&= \text{Emf}\\
\Phi_B&= \text{Magnetic Flux}\\
t&= \text{Time}
\end{aligned}
$
$
>[!Note]
>The sign of the $d\Phi_B$ follows that an increasing change in [[Magnetic Flux]] will result in a positive $d\Phi_B$, and the opposite is true for a negative.
