This is the law that allows us to find the [[Magnetic Field]] caused by highly symmetric constant [[Current]] distributions. This is given by the line integral:
$$\begin{align*}
\oint\mathbf{\vec B}\cdot d\vec l&=\mu_0(i_C+i_D)_{encl}\\
\hline\\
\mathbf{\vec B}&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
\vec l&= \text{Path}\\
\mu_0&= \text{Magnetic Constant}\\
i_C&= \text{Conduction Current}\\
i_D&= \text{Displacement Current}
\end{align*}$$
Where $i_D$ is the [[Displacement Current]] and $i_C$ is the regular conduction current. This tells us that the [[Magnetic Field]] in a closed loop is proportional the the [[Current]] contained inside of it.
