Grounding the circuit will allow us to define specific values for the [[Voltage]]. This is done by connecting the circuit to a large conductor (the earth, large pipes etc.). When connected to the large conductor, this will have a voltage of "0V". However, since the place that is grounded isn't connected anywhere else with the circuit, the voltage doesn't flow out. 
>[!Warning]
>The placement of the ground will change the reference point where the the voltage is 0.
