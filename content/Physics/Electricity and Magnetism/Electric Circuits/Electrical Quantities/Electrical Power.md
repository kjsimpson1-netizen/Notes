## DC Circuits
We can use the [[Current]] and the [[Voltage]], or the [[Resistance]] to find the overall power output from electrical circuit components:
$$\begin{align*}
P= IV&&P= \frac{V^2}R&&&P= I^2R\\
\hline
\end{align*}$$
$$\begin{align*}
P&= \text{Power}\\
V&= \text{Voltage}\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
R&= \text{Resistance}
\end{align*}$$
## AC Circuits
For [[Alternating Current]] circuits, we can use the [[Root-Mean-Square]] values for the [[Current]] and the [[Voltage]], as well as the [[Power Factor]] to find the power:
$$\begin{align*}
P_{avg}&= V_{rms}I_{rms}\cos\phi=I_{rms}^2R\\
\hline\\
P&= \text{Power}\\
V&= \text{Voltage}\\
R&= \text{Resistance}
\end{align*}$$
