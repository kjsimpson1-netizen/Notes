These are materials that under certain given critical temperatures, have 0 [[Resistance]] and therefore the same [[Current]] can flow indefinitely around a closed loop with no battery. As well as magnetic properties, these superconductors also have magnetic properties as well, one of these being the [[Meissner Effect]]
## Properties
### Type 1
When there is a diamagnetic super conductor, it will repel any normal ferromagnetic or paramagnetic materials, and this is what causes magnetic levitation. 
### Type 2
When these materials are placed under a magnetic field, the bulk of the material is still super conducting, however there are thing filaments running parallel to the [[Magnetic Field]] that will return to regular phase.
