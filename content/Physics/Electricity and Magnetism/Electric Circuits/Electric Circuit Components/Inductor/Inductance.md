This is the measurement of how strong [[Inductor]] are. It will follow the equation:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\mathcal{E}_L&= -L\frac{dI}{dt}\\
\hline\\
L&= \text{Inductance}\\
\frac{dI}{dt}&= \text{Current Flow per second}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
>[!Note]
The negative in the equation is due to the fact that the inductor will induce a backwards [[Electromotive Force]].
## Self Inductance
The section above assumed that the two coils were parts of different circuits, however if it were in the same circuit, the magnetic field would resist the change in it's own [[Current]]. The units of self inductance, also called just inductance, is the Henry, and can be expressed by the following:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
L&= \frac{N\Phi_B}i\\
\hline\\
i&= \text{Current}\\
N&= \text{Number of Turns}\\
\Phi&= \text{Flux}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
