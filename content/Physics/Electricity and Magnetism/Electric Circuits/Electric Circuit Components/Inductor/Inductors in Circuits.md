## Multiple Inductors
### Series
When the  inductors are wired in series, the [[Inductance]] is additive and they  can be expressed with the following quantity:
>[!Note]
>Inductors are only considered to be in series if they have the same current flowing through them
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
L_T&= \sum_{i=1}^n{L_i}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
### Parallel
When the inductors are parallel, the [[Inductance]] will be proportional to the reciprocals:
>[!Note]
>Inductors are only considered to be parallel if they have the same [[Voltage]] across them
$$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac1{L_T}&= \sum_{i=1}^n\frac1{L_i}
\end{aligned}
$$
$$
