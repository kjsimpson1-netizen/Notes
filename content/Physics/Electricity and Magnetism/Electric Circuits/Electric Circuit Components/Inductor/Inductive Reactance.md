This is a description of the self induced [[Voltage]] that opposes the change in [[Current]] through an [[Inductor]]. The larger that the inductive reactance is, the smaller that the resulting [[Current]] will be from any given [[Voltage]], and since inductive reactance is proportional to the frequency, the lower the frequency the lower that the inductive reactance will be. 
$$
\begin{aligned}
X_L&= i\omega L\\
\hline\\
X_L&= \text{Inductive Resistance}\\
\omega&= \text{Angular Frequency}\\
L&= \text{Inductance}
\end{aligned}
$$
This can then be used in [[Ohm's Law]].
