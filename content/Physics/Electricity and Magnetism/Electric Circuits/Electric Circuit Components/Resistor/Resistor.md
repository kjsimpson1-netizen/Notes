## Series
When the resistors are wired in series, the [[Resistance]] is additive and they  can be expressed with the following quantity:
>[!Note]
>Resistors are only considered to be in series if they have the same current flowing through them
$$\begin{align*}
R_T&= \sum_{i=1}^n{R_i}
\end{align*}$$
## Parallel
When the resistors are parallel, the [[Resistance]] will be proportional to the reciprocals:
>[!Note]
>Resistors are only considered to be parallel if they have the same [[Voltage]] across them
$$\begin{align*}
\frac1{R_T}&= \sum_{i=1}^n\frac1{R_i}
\end{align*}$$
## Resistors in an AC Circuit
Resistors in an [[Alternating Current]] circuit work the exact same as [[Resistor]] in a [[Direct Current]] circuit, by following [[Ohm's Law]]. They also have the same [[Phase Angle]] as the true [[Current]].
