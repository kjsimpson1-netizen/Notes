This is an [[Operational Amplifier|Amplifier]] where the output is an amplified difference of signals coming in. Both of the signals will be measured from a common [[Grounding|Ground]], and the output will be measured against the same [[Grounding|Ground]].
$
\begin{aligned}
V_\text{out}=A(V_1-V_2)
\end{aligned}
$
