This is the same as [[Inductive Reactance]], however with [[Capacitor]] instead. The capacitive reactance can be given by the following:
$$\begin{align*}
X_C&= \frac{-i}{\omega C}\\
\hline\\
X_C&= \text{Capacitive Reactance}\\
\omega&= \text{Angular Frequency}\\
C&= \text{Capacitance}
\end{align*}$$
Which can also be used in [[Ohm's Law]] to find the voltage drop across a capacitor.
