Ohm stated that circuits that are "Ohmic" have a direct proportionality between the [[Current]], [[Voltage]] and [[Resistance]]. Things that are "non-ohmic" have a [[Voltage]] versus current graph that is non-linear. 
$
$
\begin{aligned}
I&= \frac VR=\frac VX\\
\hline\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
V&= \text{Voltage}\\
R&= \text{Resistance}\\
X&= \text{Reactance}
\end{aligned}
$
$
If the substance is nonohmic, then there isn't a direct linear proportionality between the [[Current Density]] and the [[Electric Field]] and must be represented by a more complicated function.
