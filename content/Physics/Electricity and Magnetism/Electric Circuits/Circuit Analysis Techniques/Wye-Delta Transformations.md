These are [[Resistor|Resistors]] arranged in such a way that they are neither in [[Parallel Circuits|Parallel]] nor in [[Series Circuits|Series]]. This is a way to analyze such circumstances. 
![[Wye-Delta.png]]
## Wye Network
This a resistor arranged in such a way that they form a "Y" shape. 
## Delta Circuit
These look like a triangle or resistor with nodes on all sides. 

---
We can convert between these two forms using the following equations.
## Delta to Wye
$
$
\begin{aligned}
R_1&= \frac{R_bR_c}{R_a+R_b+R_c}\\
R_2&= \frac{R_aR_c}{R_a+R_b+R_c}\\
R_3&= \frac{R_aR_b}{R_a+R_b+R_c}
\end{aligned}
$
