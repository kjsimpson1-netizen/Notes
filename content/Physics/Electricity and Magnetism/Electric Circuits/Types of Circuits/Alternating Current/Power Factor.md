This is efficiency of an [[Alternating Current]] circuit, where $\phi$ is equal to the [[Phase Angle]]. It is expressed by:
$
\begin{aligned}
\cos(\phi)&= \frac RZ\\
\hline\\
\phi&= \text{Phase Angle}\\
R&= \text{Resistance}\\
Z&= \text{Impedance}
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>This can also be used to find the [[Phase Angle]] of the circuit, however it will give the absolute value and will not say whether or not the current lags or leads
