This is the way in which an [[Alternating Current]] circuit will respond different to different frequencies. The peaking of the [[Current]] amplitude will occur at the resonance angular frequency of the circuit given by:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\omega_0&= \frac1{\sqrt{LC}}\\
\hline\\
\omega_0&= \text{Resonance Frequency}\\
L&= \text{Inducatance}\\
C&= \text{Capacitance}
\end{aligned}
$$
This is the same as the natural angular frequency of an [[L-C Circuits]], as this is when the [[Capacitor]] and [[Inductor]] are completely out of phase.
