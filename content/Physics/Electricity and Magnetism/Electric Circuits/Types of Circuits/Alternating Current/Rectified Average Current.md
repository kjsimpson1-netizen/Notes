Because the [[Current]] oscillates in an [[Alternating Current]] circuit, if you attempt to measure the [[Current]] with a regular galvanometer, it will just give the average reading of 0. 
To rectify this, we use a special kind circuit to measure the rectified average current, meaning it measures the average of only the positive half of the sine wave. The equation to go from the rectified average current to the magnitude of the current is:
$
$
\begin{aligned}
I_{rav}&= \frac2\pi I\\
\hline\\
I_{rav}&= \text{Rectified Current}\\
I&= \text{Current Amplitude}
\end{aligned}
$
$
