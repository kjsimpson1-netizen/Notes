This kind of averaging is another method to determine the actual amplitude of the [[Current]] flowing through the wire. The equation to go from the Root-Mean-Square current to the current amplitude is:
$$
\begin{aligned}
I_{rms}&= \frac I{\sqrt{2}}\\
\hline\\
I_{rms}&= \text{RMS Current}\\
I&= \text{Current Amplitude}
\end{aligned}
$$
This also works with the [[Voltage]]:
$$
\begin{aligned}
V_{rms}&= \frac V{\sqrt{2}}\\
\hline\\
V_{rms}&= \text{RMS Voltage}\\
V&= \text{Voltage Amplitude}
\end{aligned}
$$
