The net force on closed loop of wire is generally 0, however the torque is generally not. This is known as a magnetic dipole, as there is a couple moment due to the two opposite forces acting upon the wire. The torque given by a [[Magnetic Field]] on a wire carrying [[Current]] is given by:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\tau&= I\vec B\times \vec A\\
\hline\\
\tau&= \text{Torque}\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
B&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
A&= \text{Coil Area}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
## Potential Energy
The potential energy of a magnetic dipole is:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
U&= IA\times\vec B\\
\hline\\
U&= \text{Potential Energy}\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
A&= \text{Area}\\
\vec B&= \text{Magnetic Field}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
