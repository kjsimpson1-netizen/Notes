### Radius
When a charged particle starts moving in a uniform [[Magnetic Field]], it will continue to move in a circle as the force is always normal to it's velocity, causing a centripetal acceleration. The radius for a given charged particle is given by:
$
\begin{aligned}
R&= \frac{mv}{|q|\,||\mathbf{\vec{B}}||}\\
\hline\\
R&= \text{Radius}\\
m&= \text{Mass}\\
v&= \text{Velocity}\\
\mathbf{\vec{B}}&= \text{Magnetic Field}
\end{aligned}
$
### Frequency
The frequency of a cyclotron is given by:
$
\begin{aligned}
f&= \frac{qB}{2\pi m}\\
\hline\\
f&= \text{Frequency}\\
q&= \text{Charge}\\
B&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
m&= \text{Mass}
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>If the velocity isn't directly perpendicular to the [[Magnetic Field]], then rather than having a perfect circular motion, there will be a helix shape, as the velocity component parallel to the field will experience no force.
