This is the force of a charge due to both the [[Magnetic Field]] and [[Electric Field]]. This is expressed by:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec F&= \pm q(\vec E+\vec v\times \vec B)\\
\hline\\
\vec F&= \text{Force}\\
q&= \text{Charge}\\
\vec E&= \text{Electric Field}\\
\vec v&= \text{Velocity}\\
\vec B&= \text{Magnetic Field}
\end{aligned}
$$
