The force felt by an [[Electric Charge]] in a [[Magnetic Field]] is given by:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
\vec{F}&= \pm q\vec{v}\times\vec {\bf{B}}\\
\hline\\
F&= \text{Force}\\
q&= \text{Charge}\\
v&= \text{Velocity}\\
B&= \text{Magnetic Field}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
>[!Note]
>Negative charges will go in the opposite direction as positive charges
