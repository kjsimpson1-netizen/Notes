This is the same concept as [[Electric Flux]], however with [[Magnetostatics]]. It is represented with the symbol "$\Phi$" and it is a scalar quantity, not a vector quantity. The units for magnetic flux are the Weber (*Wb*). The expression for the magnetic flux is
$$\begin{align*}
\Phi_B&= \iint_S\mathbf{\vec{B}} \cdot {d} \vec{\mathbf{A}}\\
\hline\\
\Phi_B&= \text{Magnetic Flux}\\
\mathbf{\vec B}&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
\vec A&= \text{Area}
\end{align*}$$
>[!Note]
>The vector direction of the area is defined to be perpendicular to it's surface.
