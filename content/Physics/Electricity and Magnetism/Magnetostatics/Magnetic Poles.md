All magnets will have a north and a south pole, and similar to how like charges repel and opposites attract, the same polarity of a magnetic will repel and opposites will attract. 
>[!Note]
>Every magnet will have 2 poles, there is no such thing as a magnet with a singular pole (known as a monopole)

When a magnet is allowed to spin freely, it will align itself with the local [[Magnetic Field]].
