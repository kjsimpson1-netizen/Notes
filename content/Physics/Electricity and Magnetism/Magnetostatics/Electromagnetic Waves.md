Due to [[Maxwell's Equations]], we can see that [[Magnetostatics]] and [[Electrostatics]] are both connected. Therefore, we can say that [[Electric Field]] and [[Magnetic Field]] are also connected. This is what causes light waves to propagate, as a alternating wave of electric and magnetic field. The direction of this light wave are perpendicular to both the [[Electric Field]] and the [[Magnetic Field]], following the right hand rule convention. This gives us:
$
\begin{aligned}
E_y= E_0\sin(kx-\omega t)\qquad B_y=B_0\sin(kx-\omega t)
\end{aligned}
$
This will give us the speed of light by relating the permittivity and permeability of free space, giving us:
$
\begin{aligned}
c&= \frac1{\sqrt{\epsilon_0\mu_0}}\\
\hline\\
c&= \text{Speed of Light}\\
\epsilon_0&= \text{Permeability}\\
\mu_0&= \text{Permittivity}
\end{aligned}
$
>[!Note]
>The direction of propagation is given by $\vec E\times\vec B$, and the magnetic and electric fields are in phase, so when one is at a maximum, the other one is as well
