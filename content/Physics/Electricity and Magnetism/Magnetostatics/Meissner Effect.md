When cooled below the critical temperature, a [[Super-Conductors|Super Conductor]]will no longer have [[Magnetic Field]] inside of them. As the [[Magnetic Field]] in the material goes to 0, this expulsion of [[Magnetic Flux]] is called the Meissner effect. However there is still a [[Magnetic Field]] outside of the material, which will have a higher density, as the magnetic field lines must be grouped closer together.
