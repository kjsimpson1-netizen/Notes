These are [[Solenoids]] that are wrapped into a circular shape. This will then have a continuous [[Magnetic Field]] through the inside of the "donut". The magnetic field here will run perpendicularly to the radial lines. We can express the magnitude of the field by:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
B_t&= \frac{\mu_0IN}{2\pi r}\\
\hline\\
B&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
N&= \text{Windings}\\
r&= \text{Radius}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
>[!Note]
>The [[Magnetic Field]] is approximately zero outside of the coil
