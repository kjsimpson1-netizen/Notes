These are circular loops of wire that carry a [[Current]] and therefore produce a [[Magnetic Field]]. The magnitude of the [[Magnetic Field]] given by a coil is given by:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
B_x&= \frac{\mu_0Ia^2}{2(x^2+a^2)^\frac32}\\
\hline\\
B&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
\mu_0&= \text{Magnetic Constant}\\
a&= \text{Radius of Loop}\\
x&= \text{Distance along Axis}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
The maximum [[Magnetic Field]] will be given at the center of the field where $x=0$, and we can also account for a multitude of coils, giving us:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
B_x&= \frac{\mu_0NI}{2a}\\
\hline\\
B&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
N&= \text{Coil Loops}\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
a&= \text{Radius}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
>[!Note]
>The direction of the magnetic field can be determined using the right hand rule.

We can also express this as:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
B_{in}&= \mu_0 In\\
\hline\\
B&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
n&= \text{Coils}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
