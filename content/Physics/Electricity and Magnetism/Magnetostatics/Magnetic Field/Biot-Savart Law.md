This is the law that is the basis of the equations for [[Magnetic Field]]. It is given by:
$$
$$\begin{aligned}
d\mathbf{\vec B}&= \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\cdot\frac{I\,d\vec l\times \hat r}{r^2}\\
\vec B&= \frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi}\oint_C\frac{d\vec l\times \hat r}{r^2}\\
\hline\\
\mathbf{\vec B}&= \text{Magnetic Field}\\
\mu_0&= \text{Magnetic Constant}\\
I&= \text{Current}\\
\vec l&= \text{Wire Length}\\
\hat r&= \text{Radius Unit Vector}\\
r&= \text{Distance}
\end{aligned}$$
$$
This states that an infinitesimally small portion of the [[Magnetic Field]] is proportional to an infinitesimally small length of the wire multiplied by the constant $\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi r^2}$.
